Your Best Health

Personalized, Patient-Centered Care

All of our primary care clinics have been formally recognized as patient-centered medical homes. This model of care emphasizes shared decision-making with you, and helpful communication and coordination support among all of your health care providers.

It’s an approach designed to help you make more informed choices – and receive the best results.

Learn more about our patient care

World-Class Expertise When You Need It Most

As Sacramento’s No. 1 hospital, we’re proud to offer the extraordinary expertise of our region’s only academic medical center.

By connecting you to a health care partner who delivers personalized primary care and world-class specialty care, better health for you and your family has never been easier.
