Inspiration

Located in sunny Southern California, our scenic campus sits on 1,474 acres of lush, rolling hills just a few miles from the sparkling Pacific Ocean and borders the beach cities of Laguna Beach and Newport Beach. With state-of-the-art research facilities, vibrant student life, and abundant natural beauty, UC Irvine offers the perfect environment to grow, thrive, and achieve success in your graduate journey. On and off campus, you have access to unmatched cultural, recreational, and professional opportunities that will enrich both your academic journey and personal growth.

diversity

Leader in Inclusive Excellence

At UC Irvine, we take pride in being a Leader in Inclusive Excellence. Our campus thrives with an inclusive student body, welcoming individuals from every state in the U.S. and over 65 countries, representing a wide range of interests, backgrounds, and experiences—reflecting a truly global community. We are committed to creating an environment where every individual feels valued and empowered. Recognized for our exceptional commitment to inclusivity, UC Irvine ranks first for inclusivity in the 2019 Wall Street Journal/Times Higher Education rankings and No. 3 for inclusivity among top colleges in the West. Our dedication ensures that all voices are heard, and diverse perspectives are integrated into our academic and community life. Join us and be part of a university that not only celebrates inclusivity but actively fosters an enriching educational experience for everyone.
