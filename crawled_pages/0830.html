UC San Diego Alumni

As a UC San Diego graduate, you are automatically a part of the Alumni Association. No fees, no need to sign-up. All the perks. We're your resource to stay connected to the university and each other, to assist you as you grow professionally and inspire the next generation of alumni.

Alumni stories

Meet exceptional individuals who define our alumni community. These spotlights celebrate their achievements, passions and the lasting impact they’re making in their fields and beyond. See more alumni news.
Explorer

Kevin Leu ’02

Kevin Leu ‘02 isn’t your typical UC San Diego alum. A former television reporter turned world traveler, Kevin interviews people who have traveled to every country in the world on his podcast series. In his own travels, he’s at 100 countries and counting! 🗺️✈️ Check out the post.

Entrepreneur

Makenzie Lowe ’14

Makenzie Lowe ’14 founded The Good Loop, transforming simple clothing swaps into a scalable, community-driven model for sustainability, inclusion and social good. ♻️👕🌱 Read her story. 📸📹

Honoree

Ann Crady Weiss ’93

2026 Illustrious Alumni Award honoree, Ann Crady Weiss ’93 is co-founder and CEO of Hatch, whose products have helped millions build healthier sleep habits from newborns to adults. 🌙✨Watch her inspiring story now.
