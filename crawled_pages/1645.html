About us

University of California Health comprises six academic health centers, 21 health professional schools, a Global Health Institute and systemwide services that improve the health of patients and the University’s students, faculty and employees. All of UC’s hospitals are ranked among the best in California and its medical schools and health professional schools are nationally ranked in their respective areas.

    UCSF Fresno is caring for communities beyond hospital and clinic walls. UCSF Fresno Mobile HeaL was in San Joaquin today providing health screenings and services as part of a Health & Resource Fair organized by Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria.     Early detection is increasingly recognized as one of the most effective strategies for improving health outcomes. Leveraging real-world data from the UC Health Data Warehouse, researchers across UC’s academic health system — including teams at UCSF Bakar Computational Health Sciences Institute, David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and UC San Diego School of Medicine — are identifying disease risk earlier and advancing more personalized, data-driven care. Recent research highlights include 🔬 • Identifying disease trajectories in a neurodegenerative disorder • Precision approaches to Alzheimer’s disease detection and treatment • Predicting and preventing hospital delirium • Using AI to anticipate cancer relapse Thanks to CDI2’s outreach and engagement, the number of UC researchers using the platform has increased by 113% over the past year. Since 2020, this work has led to 62 publications, including 16 in 2025. Together, these efforts underscore the impact of data-driven research—and the critical role of federal science agencies, including the National Institutes of Health, in supporting this work. 🔗 Read more: https://bit.ly/4msUS6w #DataDrivenCare #PrecisionHealth #PublicHealth #NIH
