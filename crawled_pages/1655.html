Health Care Professionals

We train the health care providers of tomorrow, today, offering professionals the knowledge, skills and abilities to deliver exemplary care.

Referring Physicians

At UC Davis Health we’re proud to offer referring physicians and their patients a truly comprehensive array of clinical services and educational resources. All of this is delivered by exceptional providers and grounded in the latest medical knowledge and research.

We place special focus on patient-centered care and maintaining a respectful, culturally and linguistically sensitive health care environment for our patients.

Learn about patient referrals and transfers

Career Opportunities

Our academic health system includes one of the country’s best medical schools, a nationally ranked 627-bed acute-care teaching hospital, and a 1,000-member physicians’ practice group. UC Davis Health is a hub of innovation that encompasses our primary and specialty care clinics, UC Davis Medical Center, UC Davis School of Medicine, and The Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing.

Explore careers at UC Davis Health

Residency Programs and Fellowships

Our nationally recognized residency and fellowship programs and world-class faculty seek to prepare our residents and fellows to excel. Our programs include hands-on patient experience at our nationally recognized UC Davis Medical Center and in our many ambulatory care settings.

View all programs

Professional Development

We encourage our health care community to enhance their skills and continue growing as individuals and leaders. We have a wide array of continuing education options to stay up to date on the latest health care trends and research.
