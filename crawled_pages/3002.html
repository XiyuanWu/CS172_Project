Learning & Development

UCOP Learning & Development supports all employees to learn skills that equip them for success in their current and future roles, connect with others across the organization, and grow personally and professionally in service of the UC mission.

Our services include:

  • Training curriculum
  • Cohort learning
  • Consultation support on learning and development needs
  • UCOP Learning Center
  • On-demand learning
  • Partnerships with departments
  • Data and reporting for HR functions
  • Performance evaluation system and process
  • Team development
