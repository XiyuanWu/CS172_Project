Welcome to the DSC!

At the University of California, Irvine, providing a culture of inclusion and equal opportunity for students with disabilities is a campus wide responsibility and commitment. UCI demonstrates its core values of individual growth, development, civility, and diversity by recognizing students with disabilities as an important part of its student body. Our mission is to empower students to maximize their abilities to thrive in today’s global community.

 

NEW STUDENT AND INSTRUCTOR PORTAL 

The Disability Services Center has released a new and improved MyDSC instructor and student portal via Accommodate. You can use the MyDSC portal to: 

 Send accommodation letters to your instructors, using the Quarter Request feature. 

Schedule appointments with your DSC counselor and other DSC staff members. 

Make reservations to take in-person exams through the DSC. 

Access Ring Road Rides. 

 Please visit the MyDSC portal for students and instructors and make sure to bookmark it as a favorite.   

 

Register with DSC

New to DSC? Registering is the first step towards getting accommodation services at UCI.

Access MyDSC

My DSC is your portal to activating your DSC approved accommodations.

Ring Road Rides

Ring Road Rides is a service provided to students with mobility limitations to help get to and from classes.

 

Report Accessibility Barriers

Accessibility on campus is everyone’s responsibility. If you know of an accessibility barrier, have a complaint, or would like to file a grievance, let us know.

 

Alternate Instructional Material

Alternate Instructional Material Services can help students with print disabilities access course materials in alternative formats. 

 

Testing Accommodations

Students with testing accommodations need to schedule their exams with the DSC. We have information on how to schedule and much more! 

 

Note-Taking Resources

Explore popular note-taking methods and resources, as well as options for support.

Contact DSC

If you have questions or comments about DSC or accessibility at UCI, call or email our office!

Assistive Technology

Learn about assistive technology (AT). AT can be equipment or software designed to address barriers encountered by a person with a disability.

 

Campus Assist List

There are many resources on and off campus for UCI students. Wellness Health & Counseling Services has an extensive list of resources and services for you to explore!

Make Your Vote Count!

DSC is a voter registration agency. To register to vote online, visit registertovote.ca.gov.

Campus Groups

CampusGroups is the official campus community platform at UCI. Connect with the DSC to learn about upcoming events and opportunities!
