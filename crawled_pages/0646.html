Introducing

FREE training for Northern San Joaquin Valley residents

Our new SkillRISE Program offers free training in Project Management, Advanced Manufacturing, or Renewable Energy if you live or work in Merced County, Stanislaus County, or San Joaquin County and are currently working or seeking to work in manufacturing or renewable energy OR if you're interested in developing your skills in these areas.

University of California Transitional Kindergarten Residency Program for Berkeley, Los Angeles, and Merced

Fast track your teaching career with our University of California Transitional Kindergarten Residency Program. You'll earn over $24K while you complete the one-year program! The residency includes placement in a regional TK worksite in the Central Valley, Los Angeles County, or the Oakland Area.
