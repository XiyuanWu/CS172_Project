Volunteer Applications on Hold

UC San Diego Health is currently not accepting new volunteer applications while we onboard summer volunteers. We anticipate reopening the application portal in June 2026 and look forward to connecting with you then. Below, please find general information about becoming a volunteer at UC San Diego Health.

We encourage you to become a volunteer at UC San Diego Health. Our volunteers play an integral role in assisting our staff in their patient care activities to support the San Diego community.

Many of our volunteers say their experiences at UC San Diego Health have been highly rewarding, personally fulfilling, and sometimes, life-changing.

Requirements for Volunteers

If you are considering applying to be a UC San Diego Health volunteer, ​you will need to:

  • Be at least age 16 and older
  • Be available to start immediately
  • Commit to a weekly four-hour shift for a total minimum of 100 hours of service over six to eight months

Volunteering FAQs

Application window: Applicants seeking a summer-only experience should apply between March 1 and April 15 and clearly note in their application that they are applying for summer only. To ensure timely processing, applications received before or after this window will not be considered.

Volunteers support patients and staff in clinical care areas with tasks such as helping with patient transport, errands, stocking, cleaning, lab deliveries and visiting with patients.

Other duties can include greeting patients and families, answering visitor questions and helping with wayfinding.

Applications are screened individually and require two professional/educational reference responses that are not from friends or family members. Volunteers are not selected on a first-come, first-served basis.

Specifically, we would like to know your availability, your motivation for volunteering, the qualities you have that you think will enhance our program, and the areas of UC San Diego Health that interest you.

Volunteers may be placed at one of our hospitals or clinics in San Diego County, depending on available volunteer opportunities.

Due to government restrictions, people in some visa classifications may not be eligible to participate in the volunteer program. Contact Volunteer Services at volsvcs@health.ucsd.edu for more information.

Initial steps: The application review and interview process can take an average of one month to complete.

Volunteer applications may take up to 10 business days to review. Your application will not be reviewed until your references have been received. Delayed reference responses will delay your application review.

You will receive an email confirming whether your application was accepted or rejected.

If your application is accepted, you must schedule a virtual interview within 5 days.

After scheduling your interview, you will receive an email with more information about how to prepare for your interview.

Onboarding steps: If you’re accepted after your interview, here are the steps required to become an official volunteer. Detailed instructions will be provided when you are accepted. Completing all the steps takes one month on average.

  • Provide immunizations/vaccination records.
  • Schedule and receive a TB test.
  • Review and sign acknowledgments and agreements.
  • Accept and pass a background check.
  • Complete online training courses.
  • Complete an in-person orientation and tour.
  • Fulfill training requirements in your volunteer department.

Donations to Help Patients at UC San Diego Health

Many service groups, youth groups, churches and individuals in the community have requested ways to assist our patients from home. We appreciate this thoughtfulness and graciously accept donations of these items. 

  • Reading glasses (new)
  • Magazines published in the last six months (unused)
  • New or unused books
  • Phone chargers (iPhone or Type-C)
  • Coloring books and colored pencils for adults
  • Blank journals 

Online gift list: You can also donate through the Amazon website.

  • Search for "Registry."
  • Then click "Search for a registry or gift list."
  • Scroll down the page to the "Find a registry or gift list" box and type the words "UCSD Health." Next to it, click the "Search by" drop-down and select "Gift List."
  • Click the "Search" button to see the "UCSD Patient Wish List."

Questions? Please email volsvcs@health.ucsd.edu or call 619-543-6370.
