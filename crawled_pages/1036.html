Our vision?
Advancing health for all.

As a global leader in dentistry, UCSF is advancing oral and overall health through paradigm-shifting research, breakthrough treatments, and the education of the next generation of dental leaders in order to provide quality care for anyone who needs it.

Serving our community with world-class dental expertise

The UCSF School of Dentistry is staffed by UCSF faculty, residents, and students, who offer care to our community, with over 125,000 patient visits annually.

Become a patient

Delivering future breakthroughs in dentistry today

UCSF Dentistry is continually shaping the future of oral health. Our expert clinicians take an integrated approach to care and are constantly advancing the standards for cutting-edge treatment.

Find a specialist

Leading the connection between oral health and overall health

As part of an academic health system, we actively collaborate with UCSF Health and UCSF’s schools of medicine, pharmacy, and nursing to conduct research about the important relationship between oral health and overall health.

See what drives us

Paradigm-shifting research

Ranked #1 in NIH dental research funding for over 25 years, UCSF School of Dentistry drives global innovation in oral health and cell and tissue biology—investigating everything from the oral microbiome to genetic influences and pioneering materials.

Explore research Support innovation

World-class education and training in a world-class city

Located in the heart of vibrant San Francisco, we are one of the world’s top-ranked dental schools, offering rigorous DDS and specialty programs, mentorship by pioneering faculty, exposure to advanced research, and real-world experience.
