Why choose UCSF Health for neurology?

Get expert, personalized care for a wide range of neurological diseases, from common conditions to rare and complex disorders. U.S. News & World Report ranks us as one of the top neurology and neurosurgery centers nationwide. We're a top-funded program by the National Institutes of Health (NIH), enabling us to conduct groundbreaking research on treatments for neurological conditions.

Our many specialty areas include cerebrovascular diseases, epilepsy, headache, ALS, neuromuscular conditions, memory disorders, multiple sclerosis (MS) and Parkinson's disease.

We're part of the Weill Institute for Neurosciences, integrating patient care and research to improve the lives of people living with neurologic diseases. We attract and train the best neurology specialists in the country.

World-class neurology care

We have expertise in treating a wide range of neurological conditions. At UCSF Health, you can find the treatment you need to manage symptoms and feel your best.

Neurology services we provide include:

Care for complex neurological disorders

People with conditions that don't follow typical patterns can find help at our Neurology Complex Diagnosis Clinic. Experts use advanced diagnostic tools to provide answers to patients experiencing unusual symptoms.

Specialized neurology services

We have deep expertise with many neurological conditions, including:

  • Epilepsy. Our level 4 Epilepsy Center provides comprehensive care for all forms of epilepsy. We specialize in treating drug-resistant epilepsy.

  • Headaches and migraines. At our Headache Center, we welcome patients with challenging headache and migraine conditions. We offer many treatment and management options and have Northern California's only inpatient headache center.

  • Memory disorders. Experts at our Memory and Aging Center provide comprehensive care for all types of cognitive disorders and dementia, including Alzheimer's disease.

  • Movement disorders. Our Movement Disorders Center uses the latest therapies to treat all types of movement disorders, including Parkinson's disease and essential tremor. We also offer a specialized Huntington's Disease and Ataxia Clinic.

  • Multiple sclerosis (MS). Our Multiple Sclerosis and Neuroinflammation Center provides comprehensive care and personalized treatment for MS and other neuroinflammatory diseases. Our focus on symptom management enables you to enhance your independence and quality of life.

  • Neurodegenerative diseases. At our ALS and Neurodegenerative Disease Center, you get complete care for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and other progressive neurological conditions.

  • Neuromuscular disorders. Our Neuromuscular Clinic provides comprehensive treatment for conditions affecting muscle movements and communication between nerves and the brain.

  • Traumatic brain injury. At our Neurorecovery Clinic, experts deliver ongoing care for people with traumatic brain injuries (TBIs) and other brain and spinal cord conditions.

  • Stroke. Our Comprehensive Stroke Center is part of the largest stroke and cerebrovascular program in California. We used the most advanced diagnostic techniques and treatments.

Accreditations & memberships

  • Certified comprehensive stroke center

    The Joint Commission named us a Certified Comprehensive Stroke Center.

  • Comprehensive MS care center

    We're designated a comprehensive care center and a Partner in MS Care by the National Multiple Sclerosis Society.

  • Level 4 epilepsy center

    We’re a level 4 (highest level) epilepsy center, accredited by the National Association of Epilepsy Centers.

  • Certified ALS treatment center

    We're an ALS Association-certified treatment center for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS).

  • NIH StrokeNet member

    We're part of the National Institutes of Health's network of regional leaders in comprehensive care and research on stroke prevention, treatment and recovery.
