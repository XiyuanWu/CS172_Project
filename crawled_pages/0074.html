Ignite Your Passion. Shape Your Future

The UCDC Internship Experience

UCDC connects students from all majors with dynamic internships and coursework that align with their passions. In Washington, DC, you'll gain firsthand exposure to the political process while developing critical skills in research, advocacy, and decision-making. This experience will equip you with the skills and connections needed to thrive in your career.

Launch Your Career

Explore potential career paths, gain practical experience, and build your professional network through a high-quality internship.

Immersive DC Experience

Experience life in a dynamic city, engage with its rich culture, and build lasting friendships while earning full-time academic credit.

Unparalleled Support

Comprehensive resources, including guaranteed housing, internship placement assistance, and a dedicated support network to help you thrive.

Make a Lasting Impact

Join a community devoted to personal and professional growth, and leave UCDC prepared to make a meaningful difference in the world.
