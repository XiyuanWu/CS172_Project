Residency and Fellowship Programs

Our nationally recognized residency and fellowship programs and world-class faculty seek to optimally prepare our residents and fellows to excel at our nationally recognized academic medical center and in our many ambulatory care settings.

UC Davis School of Medicine offers a variety of residency and fellowship programs through our clinical departments. View available programs by visiting the department pages listed below.
