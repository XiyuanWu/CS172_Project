UCSF.edu Feedback

Please submit suggestions and feedback to the UCSF Office of Communications web team about the www.UCSF.edu website only. For feedback about other UCSF websites, see the contact information listed on those websites.

This form is not intended for general comments or feedback about the University, its programs, or its personnel.

  • If you have feedback regarding UCSF Health and its website, please see the UCSFHealth.org contact page.
  • If you have feedback regarding a patient care experience, please contact UCSF Health’s Patient Relations.
  • If you have other feedback, please contact the appropriate department or group directly. Here is our contacts page.
