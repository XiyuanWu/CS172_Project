UCEAP Alumni and Friends

The moment you returned from studying abroad, you became a member of a growing worldwide community of 130,000 UCEAP alumni. Whether you want to take the next step in your career, simply catch up with old friends, get involved as a volunteer, or give back to current UC students with scholarship support, you'll find many ways to connect and join the conversation.

We look forward to seeing you soon!
