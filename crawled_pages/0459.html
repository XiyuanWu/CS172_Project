UCLA's Economic Impact

A report by Beacon Economics, one of California’s leading economic research and forecasting firms, shows that UCLA is responsible for over $11 billion of total economic impact on the state of California, including $4 billion of economic impact in Los Angeles. The report also shows that start-up valuations built on UCLA’s technologies since 2000 total $33 billion.
