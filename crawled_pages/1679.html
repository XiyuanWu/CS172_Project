Contact media relations

For help with media requests, please contact the Department of Public Affairs & Marketing or call one of the public information officers listed below.

Monday – Friday, 8 a.m. – 5 p.m.

Email publicaffairs@health.ucdavis.eduCall 916-734-9040

After business hours or on holidays or weekends

Email publicaffairs@health.ucdavis.edu

For urgent media questions, call the hospital operator at 916-734-2011 and ask for the public information officer who is on call.
