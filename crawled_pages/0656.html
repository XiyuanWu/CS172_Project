Welcome to Sponsored Projects Office

Sponsored Projects Office (SPO)

The Sponsored Projects Office (SPO) at UC Merced is home to the campus-delegated Authorized Organizational Representative (AOR). SPO is the official liaison between the university and sponsoring agencies, including federal, state, local, foundation, industry, and other public and private sources.

SPO staff support the preparation, review, authorization, and submission of proposals for research, training, and other sponsored activities. SPO Contract and Grant Officers are responsible for negotiating and accepting awards.
