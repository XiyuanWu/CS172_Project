UC Davis Health Executive Leadership

  • Mark Rosenblatt, M.D., Ph.D., M.B.A., M.H.A.

    Vice Chancellor of Human Health Sciences

    Rosenblatt oversees all clinical operations in the health system and is responsible for the strategic direction of UC Davis Health, including oversight of the UC Davis School of Medicine and Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Michael Condrin, M.B.A.

    Interim Chief Executive Officer for UC Davis Health

    Condrin provides strategic leadership to ensure UC Davis Health’s mission and vision are reflected in both short-term priorities and long-range goals. He oversees financial performance, hospital and clinical operations, and system-wide initiatives that enhance efficiency, patient care outcomes, and organizational effectiveness. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Debbie Aizenberg, M.D., M.B.A.

    Chief Physician Executive, UC Davis Medical Group and Chief Medical Officer for Ambulatory Care, UC Davis Health

    Aizenberg oversees physician practice operations and assists in developing the Group’s overall vision and strategic direction. She also serves as the chief medical officer for Ambulatory Care at UC Davis Health.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Ron Amodeo

    Chief Strategy Officer

    Amodeo works closely with the UC Davis Medical Group, the Medical Center, the School of Medicine, Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing, and with university leadership to guide, develop, and execute strategic and business plans across the entire clinical enterprise.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Kimberly Bleichner-Jones, M.B.A., F.A.C.H.E.

    Interim Chief Operating Officer

    Bleichner-Jones oversees operational leadership across UC Davis Health’s hospital and ambulatory services. This includes UC Davis Medical Center and UC Davis Children’s Hospital, an acute care academic medical center providing a full range of inpatient acute and intensive care services, as well as the Comprehensive Cancer Center, the Transplant Center, and more than 70 outpatient clinics throughout Northern California. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Stephen J. Cavanagh, Ph.D., M.P.A., R.N., F.A.C.H.E., F.A.A.N.

    Dean, Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing

    Cavanagh oversees one of the nation’s top 25 graduate nursing schools, with approximately 50 full-time faculty and nearly 300 students.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • John Cook

    Associate Chief Information Officer, Information Technology Department

    Cook provides leadership and vision for the Information Technology (IT) department and oversees more than 550 employees who support UC Davis Health’s overall mission and strategy.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Ryan Davis, J.D., LL.M.

    Chief Compliance and Privacy Officer

    Davis oversees the direction and leadership of the Compliance and Privacy Program, ensuring adherence to regulatory, billing, coding, and research compliance and federal (HIPAA) and state (CMIA and IPA) privacy compliance.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Jennifer Doll, CPA, M.P.A.

    Chief Financial Officer

    Doll provides executive oversight for the overall financial operation of UC Davis Health, which includes the School of Medicine, Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing, Medical Center, and the Medical Group.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Nick Eversole, M.B.A.

    Assistant Vice Chancellor, Human Health Sciences and Chief of Staff

    Eversole oversees initiatives, projects, programs, recruitments and other priorities of interest to the Vice Chancellor of Human Health Sciences. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Joseph M. Galante, M.D., M.B.A., F.A.C.S.

    Chief Medical Officer, UC Davis Medical Center

    Galante oversees Hospital Clinical Care Services, leading and directing the professional portion of the medical center’s clinical delivery system. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Bruce Lee Hall, M.D., Ph.D., M.B.A.

    Chief Clinical Officer

    Hall oversees all clinical operations in the health system, which includes creating and activating service lines, addressing total costs of care, assuring consistent, high-quality care across all venues, analyzing acquisitions of new care or post-acute care facilities, and improving overall access to care. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Tammy Kenber, M.S.

    Chief Human Resources Officer

    Kenber leads all operational departments in Human Resources across both UC Davis and UC Davis Health.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Susan Murin, M.D., MSc., M.B.A.

    Dean, UC Davis School of Medicine

    Murin leads the UC Davis School of Medicine in its mission areas of education, research, clinical care, and community partnership. During her nearly 30-year tenure at the UC Davis School of Medicine, she has held numerous prominent leadership roles both within the medical school and at UC Davis Health.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Anna Orlowski

    Chief Counsel

    Orlowski provides advice and counsel in the development of policy, procedure and strategy to address a wide range of issues related to the management and operation of the organization.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Chong Porter

    Senior Associate Vice Chancellor for Health Sciences Development and Alumni Relations and Campaign Co-Chair

    Porter is responsible for enhancing the culture of philanthropy at UC Davis Health and expanding the base of donor support through alumni relations and community engagement.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Steve Telliano, M.A.

    Assistant Vice Chancellor, Strategic Communications

    Telliano serves as the academic health system’s chief communications officer, responsible for strategy and professional staff in media relations, marketing, brand management, digital outreach, internal communications and related areas.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Hendry Ton, M.D., M.S.

    Associate Vice Chancellor for Health Equity by Design for Inclusive Excellence

    Ton leads the dedicated teams that include the Office of Health Equity by Design for Inclusive Excellence (HEDI), the Office of Medical Pathways, and the Center for Reducing Health Disparities. These teams work to create a climate of inclusion reflected in the health system’s structures, policies, practices, and relationships through transformative education, community partnerships, and collective impact.

    Read biographyarrow_forward

  • Christine Williams, R.N., M.S., C.N.S.

    Chief Nursing Executive and Chief Nursing Informatics Officer

    Williams serves as the senior leader responsible for guiding nursing strategy and operations, ensuring excellence in patient care, advancing professional development, and representing nursing leadership in executive decision-making. 

    Read biographyarrow_forward
