Digital Accessibility Compliance Project

UCSF is committed to protecting the civil rights of every individual and fostering a culture of full inclusion and belonging. We aim to ensure that everyone can access, benefit from, and contribute to our digital information, programs, and services. New federal rules requiring compliance with WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA take effect on May 11, 2026. By then, all content owners will need to prioritize, assess, and fix websites, applications, and content to ensure compliance. More details in the April 22, 2026 UCnet article Update on digital accessibility compliance.
