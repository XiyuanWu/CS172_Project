Report a Digital Accessibility Concern

UC San Diego is committed to making strides toward accessibility for everyone.

  • Students looking for accommodations, please contact the Office for Students with Disabilities.
  • Faculty, staff and campus visitors wanting to learn about UC San Diego resources and services should visit the Disability Resources site.

For concerns regarding digital accessibility at UC San Diego, please fill out the form below.

Note

Digital content might include (but is not limited to) the following:

  • Websites
  • Videos
  • Course material
  • Email
