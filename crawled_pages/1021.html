Locations

With more than 20 locations throughout San Francisco and beyond, UC San Francisco is an industry of its own, contributing greatly to the intellectual vigor and economic vitality of the City by the Bay. 

If you’re traveling to one of UCSF’s main campuses, affiliated hospitals or independent sites, there are site-specific maps below.

Map of main locations

Map of hospitals and clinics

 

Parnassus Heights

The original UCSF campus located at Parnassus Heights is made up of a tight network of buildings where faculty, staff, students and others are engaged in patient care, research and education activities. Covering almost six blocks, the campus serves as headquarters for UCSF leaders.

Map and directions to the Parnassus Heights campus

Mission Bay

UCSF Mission Bay is a teaching, research and clinical care campus with a daily population of about 3,500 UCSF faculty, staff, students, patients and other visitors. With more than 1 million square feet of research space, the thriving campus also includes an integrated hospital that serves women, children and cancer patients.

Map and directions of the Mission Bay campus

Mount Zion

The UCSF Mount Zion campus is a hub of specialized medical center clinics and surgery services located in the Western Addition. In addition to housing the UCSF Helen Diller Family Comprehensive Cancer Center, Mount Zion boasts a growing network of outpatient services.

Map and directions of the Mount Zion campus

Mount Sutro Open Space Reserve

Mount Sutro is home to a vibrant, 61-acre open space reserve owned by UCSF as well as campus housing nestled amongst the trees in the heart of San Francisco. The Reserve features more than 5 miles of public, multi-use trails, with a 900-foot elevation gain, that cater year-round to hikers, trail runners, dogs on leash and cyclists.

Map and directions to the Mount Sutro Open Space Reserve

About the location of the Mount Sutro Open Space Reserve

UCSF Fresno

The UCSF Fresno Medical Education Program is a branch of the School of Medicine, extending critical physician training and medical care to the San Joaquin Valley. Established in 1975, it’s the largest physician-training program between Sacramento and Los Angeles, with more than 300 medical residents and fellows and more than 300 visiting medical students each year.

Map and directions to UCSF Fresno

About the location of UCSF Fresno

UCSF Affiliates

UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospital Oakland

Affiliated with UCSF since 2014, the hospital has over 30 pediatric specialties and is one of only five Level 1 Pediatric Trauma Centers in California. Its research arm, Children’s Hospital Oakland Research Institute, is one of the top pediatric research centers in terms of National Institutes of Health grant funding.

Map and directions to UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital Oakland

About the affiliation with UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital Oakland

San Francisco VA Medical Center

The SFVAMC provides primary medical care, comprehensive homeless center, psychosocial and health care services. It has been affiliated with UCSF for over 50 years. All physicians are jointly recruited by SFVAMC and the UCSF School of Medicine.

Map and directions to the San Francisco VA Medical Center

About the affiliation with the San Francisco VA Medical Center

Zuckerberg San Francisco General

Since 1864, Zuckerberg San Francisco General and the UCSF School of Medicine have worked in close collaboration to provide health care services for the people of San Francisco. ZSFG is one of UCSF’s primary teaching hospitals, where medical residents train under UCSF faculty and City staff.

Map and directions to Zuckerberg San Francisco General

About the affiliation with Zuckerberg San Francisco General

Gladstone Institutes

An independent, nonprofit biomedical research institution comprising three institutes devoted to the study of cardiovascular disease, HIV/AIDS, and Alzheimer’s disease and other neurological disorders. Through the institutes’ affiliation with UCSF, Gladstone investigators hold UCSF faculty appointments and participate in many University activities, including the teaching and training of graduate students.
