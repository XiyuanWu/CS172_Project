Contribute to Medical Discoveries

Volunteer for research at UC Davis and contribute to discoveries that may improve health care for you, your family, and your community.

We conduct more than 2,000 research studies at any given time, including basic science, translational, and clinical trial research — all with the goal of bringing new, effective and safe treatments to patients more quickly.
