A campus full of ways to belong and connect

Spend a little time at any of the 10 colleges, and you might forget that UCSC is a large research university. That focus on small-scale community is intentional. From the Principles of Community that we all strive to uphold to our unique college system, we want every student to feel welcome, supported, and a sense of belonging during their time on campus. 

Learn about connection and belonging at UCSC

Helping Banana Slugs thrive

We believe that holistic well-being is foundational to student success. Just like Ariolimax dolichophallus (aka Sammy Slug) needs a healthy forest to thrive, we recognize that you need support for your physical, social and emotional well-being in order to succeed academically and personally.

Learn about health and well-being at UCSC

A division dedicated to student success

Students are the reason for our work. Supporting student success is paramount to the programs we offer, the events we host, the resources we share, and the initiatives we lead. We are fierce advocates for marginalized students and champion access to equitable UCSC experiences for all.
