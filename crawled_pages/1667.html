Browse Services and Specialties A-Z

Our specialists are available when the need arises. To learn more about a specific service or specialty, please use the alphabetized list below. You can search by a general term or by the medical name for the specialty.

Most specialists require a referral from a physician in the community. For information on the referral process, contact the Consumer Resource Center toll free at 800-2-UCDAVIS (800-282-3284), Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
