About This Site

This site serves as UC San Diego’s central resource for timely updates on major issues that impact our campus community. Our goal is to provide faculty, staff and students with clear, accessible updates and resources in one place. For updates about emergencies affecting campus directly, please visit emergency.ucsd.edu.
