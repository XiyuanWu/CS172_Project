Graduate Division Staff Directory

Our dedicated team, led by our Associate Vice Chancellor for Graduate Affairs and Anne and Michael Towbes Graduate Dean, works together to provide academic services, financial support resources, admissions and outreach programming, and professional development for all our graduate students here at UC Santa Barbara. 

Staff are available for consultations or advising via Zoom.

Please contact the relevant staff member to schedule a virtual meeting. Click on the staff member's name in the directory to find out more about their role at the Graduate Division.
