Systemwide Office of Civil Rights

The University of California strives to be a fair and just learning, working, and living environment and we must be responsive when issues arise that hinder us from achieving that goal.

The Systemwide Office of Civil Rights – composed of the Systemwide Title IX Office, Systemwide Anti-Discrimination Office, and Systemwide Disability Rights Office – provides systemwide guidance and support on issues related to protecting civil rights at UC’s 10 campuses, medical centers, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL), and the UC Office of the President. We ensure uniform interpretation and consistent implementation of two comprehensive systemwide policies: the Sexual Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy and the Anti-Discrimation Policy. SOCR also provides leadership, training, education, and investigative support for UC offices responsible for protecting all civil rights.The location Title IX Officers, Local Implementation Officers, and Title VI/VII Officers have a dual reporting line to the Executive Director of SOCR.

Systemwide Title IX Office

The Systemwide Title IX Office addresses the university’s obligation to prevent and address sex discrimination, including sexual violence and sexual harassment, in all university programs and activities.

Systemwide Anti-Discrimination Office

The Anti-Discrimination Office oversees the university’s responsibilities and procedures as set forth in the Anti-Discrimination Policy related to discrimination and harassment on the basis of protected categories and related retaliation to ensure equitable and inclusive education, employment, and patient care environments.

Systemwide Disability Rights Office

The Systemwide Disability Rights Office in SOCR assists in implementing systemwide initiatives and best practices to address compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act and Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act and other federal and state laws.
