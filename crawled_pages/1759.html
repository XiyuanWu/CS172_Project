Our reputation for top quality health care and our beautiful location on the Pacific Ocean attract patients from around the world to UC San Diego Health.

Known for outstanding, culturally competent patient care, patients' families also enjoy the cultural and recreational attractions that make San Diego a popular tourist destination, famous for its year-round mild climate.

World-Class Care for Patients Worldwide

If you have a complex condition, you need expert care. Make UC San Diego Health the first stop in your health care journey. You'll find a suite of personalized services to help you navigate international health care and travel to our facilities in San Diego, California, USA.

World-Class Care with Personalized Services

Your patient coordinator is your advocate, helping you access all the exceptional care and services you expect from a world-class hospital. We offer multilingual concierge services to guide you on your journey. We assist you before your visit, during your treatment in San Diego, and with coordinating follow-up care with your doctor back home. Contact our team today for information on how we can make your medical travel effective and efficient to get you back home and healthy.

Matching your medical needs with the best UC San Diego Health physician(s)

Arranging routine or specialty appointments and inpatient admissions

Scheduling comprehensive physicals that include VIP service

Arranging for medical records to be reviewed

Working with your home physician to discuss your complete medical history and arrange for a referral

Providing any necessary translation services for medical literature

Preparing a report that includes a treatment plan, an estimated length of stay and an approximate cost for your care

Follow-Up Care

When it's time for you to return home, we'll compile a package of information for your personal physician. This information will detail your follow-up care instructions, medications and a translation of the procedures performed while you were our patient. Your personal physician is welcome to contact UC San Diego Health at any time to address any concerns that may arise.

Second Opinion Medical Records Review

UC San Diego Health offers second opinion medical consults for patients and physicians outside the United States.

Our physicians and surgeons can work directly with your local physician or provide a written summary of our recommendation for your records. A video visit is possible only if the patient requesting a second opinion has a local physician who will be present during the visit.
