NOW HIRING     

We serve the UCSF community | We serve you

 

The UCSF Police Department is committed to making UCSF a safe place to live, work, study, and visit. The principles of community policing underlie everything we do. We know our capabilities and strength stem from the UCSF community’s trust in us to always do our best. We partner with agencies throughout San Francisco and neighboring communities to ensure the safety and quality of life at UCSF. We share the responsibility of keeping all of us safe within the campus community.

To promote accountability and transparency, you may find information about the operations of the UCSF Police Department on our Accountability and Transparency webpage, including compliance with the UC Community Safety Plan.

Accredited since 2008 by the Commission on Accreditation for Law Enforcement Agencies (CALEA), the UCSF Police Department welcomes comments, commendations, and suggestions from the community regarding the agency’s quality of service and candidacy for accreditation. Comments may be submitted through the CALEA Accreditation Public Comment Portal.
