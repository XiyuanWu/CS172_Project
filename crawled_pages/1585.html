Resources For Researchers

The Office of Research serves as the primary campus resource for matters related to research and technology transfer. We provide a wide range of services and tools throughout the project lifecycle to help researchers and innovators succeed.

Our common goal is to help deliver insight and new technologies that transform the world for a better tomorrow.
