We support the success and well-being of the University of California locations, faculty, staff and retirees.

Systemwide Human Resources is responsible for developing a vision, strategy and environment to engage, empower and involve the university's human capital, in the present and onwards. Our portfolio includes employee and labor relations, finance, administration, people strategy, talent and culture, and total rewards.
