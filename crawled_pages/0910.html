Resources for Bike Safety

Bicycling is a core part of campus life at UCSB, helping students, staff, and visitors move efficiently and sustainably. With thousands of riders and over seven miles of dedicated bike paths, safe and predictable habits are essential to protect both cyclists and pedestrians. Here are some quick safety tips you can follow:

  • Be predictable: Ride single-file, signal your turns, and avoid sudden stops.

  • Stay alert: Watch the path, keep at least one ear open, and avoid phone use while riding.

  • Dismount when needed: Walk your bike in pedestrian zones or near buildings to ensure equitable use of shared spaces.

  • Ride at night safely: Use front and rear lights and slow down in poorly lit areas.

  • Lock and register: Use a strong U-lock, secure at least one wheel, and register your bike with UCPD.

Resources for Raccoon Safety

Raccoons are a familiar part of life in Isla Vista and on campus. While they may look playful, raccoons are wild animals, can be unpredictable, and carry serious diseases. Understanding their behavior and taking precautions helps keep you, your pets, and the community safe.

  • Keep your distance: Never feed, touch, or approach raccoons.

  • Protect your pets: Keep cats and small dogs indoors or on a leash, especially at night.

  • Secure food and trash: Remove outdoor food sources and trash to avoid attracting raccoons.

  • Be alert at night: Raccoons are nocturnal and active after dark.

  • Respond safely: If approached, make yourself look bigger, make noise, and back away slowly. If bitten or scratched, do not wait to seek medical services. Ambulance costs are waived for UC SHIP holders.

About Student Wellbeing

Student wellbeing is integral to academic success, student development, and life satisfaction. We, in Student Affairs, are committed to building relationships with our students such that together we generate optimal conditions for their wellbeing.

How We Approach Wellbeing

 

One way in which UCSB approaches wellbeing on campus is through the Socio-Ecological Model where look at wellbeing through 4 levels:

  • Individual Efforts like educating yourself on relevant health topics
  • Interpersonal Relationships that provide social support 
  • Community Resources that provide the settings that promote wellbeing
  • Social Level Systems such as policies that improve health equity

Wellbeing Podcast

Welcome to the wellbeing podcast! Students thrive when they learn, live and achieve in an environment where they feel a sense of belonging, connectedness, and personal impact. This podcast is by students for students, to offer biweekly advice from a broad range of topics including: positive psychology, meditation, sleep, nutrition, sexual health, stress reduction, body image, and more. We love to talk to departments on campus related to wellbeing. If you’re interested in being on the podcast reach out sa-wellbeing@ucsb.edu.
