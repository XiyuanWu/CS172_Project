Where to give at UC Riverside

Chart your journey

There are countless opportunities for impact here at UC Riverside. As a prominent public research university, the world relies on UC Riverside to address the biggest issues of our time. And we rely on our community to help us fill resource gaps to meet those growing needs.

We invite you to partner with us to chart a journey of impact that's unique and meaningful to you. Whether it’s your first time giving, or you’re looking for new ways to make an impact—the bold hearts and brilliant minds of the Highlander community rely on support from community members like you to secure a greater world for our tomorrow.
