External Relations

The Division of External Relations & Strategic Communications provides multi-faceted strategic support to share UC Merced’s story both broadly and to our campus community. External Relations & Strategic Communications is comprised of several units, including: Alumni Relations, Government and Community Relations, Philanthropy and Strategic Partnerships, Protocol and Special Events, Internal Communications, Marketing, and Public Relations. The External Relations & Strategic Communications team advances the university's mission, creating relationships with individuals, corporations, and foundations to generate support for UC Merced students, faculty and programs. External Relations & Strategic Communications shares stories and produces a variety of resources that showcase the newest and most innovative campus in the University of California System.
