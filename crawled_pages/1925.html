International Services

Our patients come from all walks of life and call more than 140 different countries home.

People regularly travel from countries throughout the world to experience our unparalleled medical expertise and receive high-quality care for the most complex cases. Even before you make the journey, we strive to make your experience with UCLA Health as smooth as possible.

Our patient care coordinators will walk you through the logistics of receiving care at UCLA Health. To ensure you understand your health care, we offer in-person, telephone and video interpreter services in nearly every language.

Experience the difference a world-class care team makes. Become a patient or contact us.
