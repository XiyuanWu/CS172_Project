Spring 2026 Undergraduate Commencement Ceremonies

Golden 1 Center, Sacramento, CA

Register for Commencement

NEW FOR 2026: Access to commencement registration will be tiered and based on the number of completed academic units, as outlined below. Graduates should confirm their total completed units and mark their calendars for the date and time they become eligible to register.

  • March 2, 2026 at 10:00 AM: Graduates with 180+ Units
  • March 3, 2026 at 10:00 AM: Graduates with 160+ Units
  • March 4, 2026 at 10:00 AM: Graduates with 140+ Units

Commencement registration will be open from early March through May 29, 2026.

You must register to participate in one of the commencement ceremonies. When registering, you will be asked to choose a ceremony date from the five options below. Ceremony dates will be available on a first-come, first-served basis, so register as soon as possible.  Please note that each graduate can request up to six guest tickets. Be sure to also check the dates and times of any cultural and affinity celebrations you would like to attend before confirming your commencement ceremony date.
