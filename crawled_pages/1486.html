Science Education Program

LLNL is excited to bring students back to our site. The Fun with Science field trip program returns with new programming introducing 5th grade students to lasers and optics, the Day at the Lab tour showcases facilities such as the National Ignition Facility or bring the field trip experience to your classroom with a virtual Scientist in the Classroom visit. The Physics with Phones lessons provide remote classroom resources or explore the new Virtual Discovery Center!
