Care for you

We’re here to help with all your healthcare needs – from checkups and common concerns to the most rare and complex conditions.

  • Primary care

    We provide convenient in-person and virtual care to support your wellness and coordinate with UCSF Health specialists as needed.

  • Specialty care

    Our doctors are among the top in their fields, with highly specialized expertise and experience managing complex care.

  • Emergency & urgent care

    Our emergency and urgent care teams provide prompt, expert treatment for injuries, illness and other urgent health concerns.

  • Virtual care

    Access care from wherever you are through secure video visits and digital tools that make it easier to connect with your care team.

  • Second opinions

    For help with a complex medical issue or difficult treatment decision, discuss your case with a leading expert from UCSF Health.

  • Pediatric careOpens in a new window

    We offer compassionate care for infants, kids and teens, with top specialists experienced in both routine and complex conditions.

Awards & achievements

Recognized nationally for excellence, we pair world-class medical expertise with compassionate care to deliver the best possible outcomes for patients.

  • U.S. News best hospital

    Ranked among the top hospitals in the nation

  • Five-star CMS rating

    Awarded the highest rating for quality, safety and patient experience from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

  • Top performer in patient care

    Recognized by Vizient for leadership and quality in patient care among comprehensive academic medical centers

  • “A” safety rating

    Earned top mark of "A" in patient safety from the Leapfrog Group

  • Nursing excellence

    Awarded the “Magnet” designation for excellence in nursing practice, achieved by fewer than 10% of U.S. hospitals

  • LGBTQ+ healthcare equality leader

    Recognized by the Human Rights Campaign for dedication to inclusive policies and care for LGBTQ+ patients, visitors and employees

  • "Most Wired" award for digital health excellence

    Received the highest-level designation for digital health excellence from the College of Healthcare Information Management Executives
