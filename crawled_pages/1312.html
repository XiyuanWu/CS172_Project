Accessibility

UCOP is committed to web accessibility

If due to a disability you encounter difficulties accessing information on our website, please contact our web team via email with a detailed description of the problem so that we may assist you.

The University of California Office of the President is committed to making our websites accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. For information about our IT accessibility policy and programs, please visit the University of California Electronic Accessibility website.
