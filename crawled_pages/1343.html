UC Recruit for Academic Job Openings

UC Recruit represents the first of its kind: a common academic personnel recruitment system used by all 10 campuses, UC's Agriculture and Natural Resources (ANR) division, and the UC Office of the President.  Designed as a web-based, academic recruitment and application management tool, UC Recruit streamlines the academic hiring process and provides secure online academic employment recruitment management that supports faculty and applicants throughout the entire recruitment process.

Systemwide guidance is available regarding Contributions to DEI Statements in Academic Recruitments.

The UC Recruit Governance Board is the governing body for this systemwide collaboration; and the Board's membership is comprised of one representative for each UC location using the UC Recruit system.

Academic job openings are managed at the campus level.  Please use the links below to search each campus-specific UC Recruit site for academic jobs at each location.  Links are also provided for affiliated job sites, the Higher Education Recruitment Consortium (HERC) and the Mitratech Circa job board.
