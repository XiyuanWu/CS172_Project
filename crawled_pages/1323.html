UC Alumni Association Officers

The Alumni Associations of University of California (AAUC) is a convening space for alumni leaders across the UC system to discuss and collectively address issues impacting the UC alumni community. The leadership of AAUC is comprised of 4 alumni officers that also serve as alumni representatives to the UC Board of Regents.

 

Brian Komoto
Alumni Regent Designate, 2024-2025
Secretary of the Alumni Associations of UC

 

Geoffrey Pack
Alumni Regent, 2024-2025
Vice President of the Alumni Associations of UC

 

Alfonso Salazar
Alumni Regent, 2024-2025
President of the Alumni Associations of UC
Ann Wang
Alumni Regent Designate, 2024-2025
Treasurer of the Alumni Associations of UC
