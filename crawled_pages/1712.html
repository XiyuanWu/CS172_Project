We all have a role to play

UC Davis is composed of hundreds of thousands of people. All different. All working together toward our mission of going beyond the expected to ensure a better future for all.

As annual donors

At any size, annual gifts are some of the most meaningful ways you can donate. They have a tremendous impact across the university, and embody the spirit of service that reflects who we are as Aggies.
Learn more about annual giving

As part of your legacy

When you include UC Davis in your estate plan, your generosity will have a powerful impact—inspiring bigger ideas and bolder solutions that make a difference. We have a team of people ready to work with you to find the best charitable plan.
Learn more about planned giving

As parents

You’ve guided your child in their educational journey. Now, help us make that same journey possible for other students, too.
Learn more about parent giving

As employees

When you work at UC Davis, you already make valuable contributions to our university. And by supporting a program that aligns with your passions, you can make an even greater impact.
Learn more about employee giving

As corporate partners

From sponsored programs to internship opportunities and more, we seek out partnerships with corporations and foundations looking to create beneficial opportunities that elevate the offerings of everyone involved.
Learn more about corporate engagement

As a community

When Aggies join forces, there’s nothing we can’t do. Join a crowdfunding movement and help fund campus projects in research, student support, public service and more.
Learn more about crowdfunding

As students

Our students are at the heart of this institution. Your success is vital to our mission—and when you give your time and attention to causes you care about, you can inspire your peers and others to follow in your footsteps.
Learn more about the student foundation

As giving society members

UC Davis offers several recognition societies to thank our many loyal alumni, friends, parents, faculty and staff whose extraordinary commitment preserves and enhances our excellence in academics, research and public service.
Learn more about giving societies

As global donors

Even when you’re not in the United States, you have the power to make a world of difference.
Learn more about giving from abroad
