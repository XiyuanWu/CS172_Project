The Rady School Ranks #2 for Entrepreneurship

The Rady School of Management now ranks #2 for Entrepreneurship on the Bloomberg Businessweek Best B-Schools 2023-24. We have also moved up 17 spots from last year to rank 53rd overall in the U.S., and #24 amongst public universities.

Like the rest of UC San Diego, we believe incisive thinking and unconventional approaches are not mutually exclusive. We like it when people don't stay in their disciplinary lanes. At Rady, there's a hunger, a fearlessness and an openness to try new ideas.
