Cascadia

The enigmatic Cascadia Subduction Zone lacks the continuous seismic activity seen at most convergent plat boundaries. We use a variety of Earth imaging techniques to understand tectonic processes in the region.

Alaska

Alaska is the last frontier for Earthscope. As the dense USArray spreads across the state we start to observe the dynamic processes responsible for the region’s earthquakes and volcanoes.

Earthquake Early Warning

The origin story for today’s earthquake alert systems

MyShake

MyShake is our global smartphone seismic network. Now delivering earthquake alerts in California.

ShakeAlert is the U.S. earthquake early warning system detecting earthquakes with Berkeley’s seismic network and generating alerts.

Observational Systems

Data is at the heart of all our research activities, so we also work to design, develop and implement new geophysical observational systems, as part of the Berkeley Seismological Laboratory.
