Fee Schedule

Home / Tuition & Fees / Fee Schedule

The fee schedule provides an estimate of expected tuition and fees on a semester basis. For an explanation of specific fees, visit Fee Definitions.

Some or all instruction for part or all of the Academic Year may be delivered remotely. Tuition and fees have been set regardless of the method of instruction and will not be refunded if instruction occurs remotely for any part of the Academic Year. Figures for tuition and fees represent currently approved or proposed amounts and may not be final. Actual tuition and fees are subject to change by the University of California as determined to be necessary or appropriate. Final approved tuition and fee levels may differ from the amounts presented.

Information About Fees for Other Programs

  • Summer Session Fees are different from spring and fall semester fees. These fees can be found on the Summer Sessions website.
  • Concurrent Enrollment Fees can be found on the University Extension website.
  • Self-Supporting Graduate Degree Program fees can be found on the University of California website.
