Log into Webgrants 4 Students to confirm or change your school of attendance to UC San Diego (school code 001317). Be sure to complete any verifications or forms that are outstanding to ensure timely disbursement of your grant.

Middle Class Scholarships

Initial awarding of Middle Class Scholarship (MCS) is almost complete. The California Student Aid Commission (CSAC) requires that we continuously monitor awards for changes to eligibility. Adjustments will be made based on changes to enrollment, cost of attendance or any other factor impacting award calculation. Still missing your award? Please reach out starting in mid-February if your funds are not yet awarded.

Adding or Dropping units? This can impact your financial aid and your bill

Just a reminder, adjusting your enrollment this can impact your financial aid on TritonPay, please allow time for your bill to reflect any updates.
