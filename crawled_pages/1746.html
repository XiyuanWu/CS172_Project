Use the MyUCSDHealth app to manage your health information and communicate with your doctor on your mobile devices. With the MyUCSDHealth app you can: · Log in to your MyUCSDChart patient portal · Review test results, medications, immunization history, and more · Stay in touch with your physician · Manage your appointments · Upload health and fitness data, including data from Apple Health app, when enrolled in self-tracking programs · View and pay your bill · Access your family’s health information You can connect your account with Apple Health to integrate health-related data from your personal devices into MyUCSDChart. Please note that information in this app is not a substitute for consultation with your provider. Seek a doctor's advice in addition to using this app and before making any medical decisions.

The setup on Scripp’s MyChart has worked perfectly since day one. In addition, the UCSD app screen does not fit within the app window (too wide, lops off about 1/4 of info on right side), and also places radial buttons below or partially below the button the screen (as app screen not only to wide on home page prior to login, once logged in app screen is occasionally too long). UCSD Tech today suggested problem was likely with my phone settings. I disagreed as the Scripps MyChart works on my phone and options are set the same for both apps. I see other user reviews of UCSD’s MyChart report repetitive issues, and lower than 5-star ratings (2 to 4-stars appear frequently). Perhaps Epic’s tech support could me contacted by UCSD to explore/discover what is causing the differences in the same app under two different health care providers?
