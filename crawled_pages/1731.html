Same-Day Care Options

Select a service that matches your symptoms or condition and save your spot online. Please note:

  • Medi-Cal patients: Medi-Cal insurance is not accepted at Express Care locations or for Express Care Video Visits. If you need same-day care, please use our Urgent Care services. 
  • UC San Diego students: Please use MyStudentChart to schedule your visit.

Schedule My Video Visit

Express Care Video Visits Are For:

  • Allergies (seasonal)
  • Burns (minor)
  • Cold, cough and flu symptoms
  • Conjunctivitis (pink eye)
  • Constipation
  • COVID-19 treatment
  • Diarrhea
  • Fever
  • Insect bites and stings
  • Painful urination
  • Prescription refill*
  • Rashes
  • Sexually transmitted disease (STD) counseling
  • Sinus problems
  • Sore throat
  • Wound check

*Note: We can't prescribe controlled substances — such as narcotic and pain medicine and medications for anxiety, attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), sleep and weight loss — through this telemedicine service.

Save My Spot

Visit Express Care For:

Any condition treated at an Express Care Video Visit and:

  • Asthma (worsening conditions)
  • COVID-19 testing or symptom evaluation
  • Cuts and wound closures (minor)
  • Earaches and ear infections
  • Flu shots
  • Gynecologic complaints (minor)
  • Skin infections
  • Sprains, strains and simple fractures
  • Vomiting
  • Workplace injuries (initial workers' comp visit)

Visit Urgent Care for:

Any condition treated at an Express Care Video Visit, Express Care and:

  • Abdominal complaints (minor)
  • Allergic reactions
  • Animal bites
  • Eye injuries, infections or foreign objects in the eye
  • Fractures and sprains (moderate)

Emergency Room

Go to the ER when life or limb is at risk. Making the right choice could save your life.

If you think you might have a serious condition — including heart attack, stroke, severe bleeding, head injury or other major trauma — call 911 immediately. Do not take a chance with anything life-threatening.

Emergency Room Wait Times

As you prepare for your visit to the emergency department, please note that times reflect the approximate wait before your initial evaluation.

Emergency department wait times may change unexpectedly based on the number of patient arrivals. Wait times may not reflect the order of arrival so that patients with more serious conditions are treated first. Patients requiring hospitalization will be admitted as beds become available at any of our three hospitals.
