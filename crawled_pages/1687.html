The Principles of Community By Community. For Community.

At UC Davis, we don’t just imagine a better world—we cultivate it. Thirty-five years ago, seeds of student activism planted the foundation for something extraordinary: the Principles of Community. What began as a grassroots effort has grown into a defining ethos—a mantra deeply rooted in UC Davis excellence and the belief that every person in our Aggie community deserves to thrive.

Integration into University Life

At UC Davis, the Principles of Community are more than ideals—they’re woven into the fabric of university life. Explore training and educational opportunities designed to inspire respect, inclusion, and equity. Learn about programs that bring these values to life, fostering a culture where everyone belongs. Principles of Community Gallery of Materials

The Principles of Community belong to everyone at UC Davis. To help you celebrate, share, and live these values in your own spaces, we’ve created a gallery of materials—including posters, digital backgrounds, and the iconic Dynamo spiral. These visuals are designed to help units, teams, and individuals highlight the Principles in presentations, gatherings, and everyday communications.

Materials are free to download and customize. Some Canva templates require a paid Canva seat to edit. We’ll continue adding new materials, so check back for updates—or request customized versions to fit your unit’s needs.

For additional guidance on inclusive and accessible communications, visit the Accessibility & Inclusivity guide for communicators, created by UC Davis Strategic Communications.

All are welcome

UC Davis adheres to California's Proposition 209 which prohibits discrimination against or giving preferential treatment to any individual or group in public employment, public education, or public contracting on the basis of race, sex, color, ethnicity, or national origin. 

Inclusive Excellence communications may be developed with the support of AI tools. All content is created, reviewed, and approved by our team, guided by the UC Davis Principles of Community and our responsibility to the university community.
