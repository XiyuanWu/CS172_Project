Innovating for Massively Better Healthcare!

We invited the co-founder of Rock Health, Halle Tecco, about the evolution of the Digital Health Field as she describes it in her new book, "Massively Better Healthcare." We heard first-hand accounts on how local digital health innovations became industry leaders and publicly traded companies. We examined the essential role of UCSF frontline Innovators, such as Adam Gazzaley, who recounted the journey of his start-up, Akili, to become the first FDA approved Digital Therapeutic.
