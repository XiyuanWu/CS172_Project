Welcome UC San Diego
Students with Dependents!

 

The University of California San Diego (UCSD) Students With Dependents website is here to assist students, who may identify as a student parent, caregiver, and/or guardian, in navigating the resources available to them on and off campus.

Students with dependents have many responsibilities. The University of California San Diego supports your academic achievement amidst the unique challenges and additional responsibilities you face as a Student with Dependent(s).

Browse through the web pages in this section to become familiar with the services and resources that are available to you as a Student with Dependent(s).

Read below for how to identify as a student with a dependent.

 

Indicating Students with Dependents Status

How to indicate student with dependent status to gain access to SwD programs and resources, SwD grant, priority registration:  

  • Self-identify as SwD in ISIS: go to TritonLink and navigate to student profile/personal information to update student with dependent status  
  • SwD grant and priority registration for student parents of minor children: complete FAFSA or CADAA and submit Students with Dependents Verification form using the Document Submission Tool on TritonLink
