Mission Statement

The Division of Equity, Justice, & Inclusive Excellence builds community and capacity by promoting a model of shared responsibility for equity and inclusion, and providing education, resources, and partnership.

Vision

A campus community that both cultivates the well-being of its members and achieves excellence in learning, discovery, and service by intentionally honoring and welcoming diverse contributions and perspectives.

Values

Collaboration—working together with common purpose
Respect—treating all persons with dignity
Fairness—ensuring equity in process and outcomes for all persons
Transparency—providing clarity in communication and goals
