Partnering for a Healthier Future

Philanthropy at UC Davis Health

What will your impact be?

Academic medical centers like UC Davis Health impact the community in many ways. We educate and train physicians, residents, fellows, nurses and other health professionals to treat illnesses and injuries. We conduct innovative research that will benefit the health of both the individual and of broad communities. We encourage our clinical and research teams to think boldly and look for new solutions to existing or emerging diseases that we see, or may see, in our local and global communities. And we focus on providing culturally and linguistically sensitive care, with compassion for patients and their families. The combination of care, research and teaching equals better physical and emotional well-being for all.

UC Davis Health seeks forward-thinking philanthropic partners who share our vision for a healthier future. With your partnership, we can continue to provide exceptional medical care, forge bold new discoveries, bolster our education and training programs for future caregivers, and quickly respond to new opportunities and challenges that arise.

What will your impact be? Connect with us today to learn about the many ways you can partner with us to create a healthier future.
