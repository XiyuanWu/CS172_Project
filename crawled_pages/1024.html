Excellence at every level of care

At the UCSF Dental Center, every patient receives care guided by compassion, innovation, and excellence. From routine cleanings and X-rays to complex treatments like implants, full-mouth reconstruction, or head and neck cancer care, our clinicians provide comprehensive services using the latest technologies and backed by the expertise of one of the world’s leading academic health institutions.

Why UCSF?

Premier care by UCSF dental experts

The UCSF Dental Center draws upon the excellence of our faculty and residents to provide world-class oral health care to our local community.

Care you can trust

UCSF is a world-renowned health care and research institution. Our top priority is excellent, patient-centered care.

Experienced specialists

At UCSF’s dental clinics, our expert faculty are involved in all levels of care, so you know you’re in good hands with the foremost dental experts in the world.

A full-service practice

As a patient, you’ll have access to the full spectrum of oral care—from cleanings and x-rays to specialized treatment for complex conditions, all at the same location.

A dedicated support team

Our support staff are highly-qualified, experienced, and ready to provide you with the service you need.

Stats

Serving more patients, in more ways

Our community clinics are here to make sure everyone has access to the excellence of UCSF School of Dentistry. Here are some facts to consider as you choose a partner in health.

  • With fourteen specialty clinics across two San Francisco campuses, UCSF delivers exceptional care spanning all areas of dentistry—from preventative services to advanced specialties like orthodontics, implants, and maxillofacial surgery.

  • Each year, more than 125,000 patients trust UCSF Dental Center for their care—reflecting our deep commitment to improving the health of our community.

Find the right provider for you

Patients can choose to be seen by a faculty-supervised dental student, a resident, or a member of our faculty, all of whom provide the highest standard when it comes to patient care.

Read our FAQ

Student dentists

As the next generation of dental professionals, our students are trained and supervised by our world-class faculty. They all have at least 1-2 years of experience, and are our lowest-cost option.

Residents

These trained dental professionals have at least 2 years of experience and offer care at a reasonable cost.

Faculty

Our faculty all have 6+ years of experience practicing dentistry and are considered experts in their field.
