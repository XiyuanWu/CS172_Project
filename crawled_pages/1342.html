Health & welfare

UC offers a wide variety of health and welfare benefits, some covered by UC and some available at UC’s group rates. Find out what you’re eligible for and how costs are shared.

Retirement

UC’s primary (or required) retirement benefits provide income, with different options depending on when you were hired or rehired. Supplemental savings plans give you more opportunities to prepare for retirement. And retiree health and home benefits provide health security.

  • UC Retirement Choice (hired on or after July 1, 2016)
  • UCRP 2013 Tier (hired between July 1, 2013, and June 30, 2016)
  • UCRP 1976 Tier (hired before July 1, 2013)
