Welcome

Office Hours

We are available by telephone at 415-476-8280 and in person at MU-200W. Our hours are 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday. We look forward to serving you.

Summer Registration

Summer 2026 registration began on April 27, 2026. Read about the important deadlines.

IRS Form 1098-T

Your IRS Form 1098-T, Tuition Statement, for tax year 2025 is available. Please read how to access your Form 1098-T.

Student Portal Access for Alumni and Former Students

Alumni and former students whose UCSF student record includes their Gmail account can use their Google credentials to log in to the student portal. If you do not have a Gmail account in your student record, or if you need to update the telephone number that you encounter during the login process, please contact the Office of the Registrar. Read more about the Google login process.

Self-Service Enrollment Verification Available for Students

Current students can produce an enrollment verification in the student portal. Access this function under the “Study List & Grades” tab.  Self-service enrollment verifications are free of charge.
