About UC San Diego Health

Leading the Way in San Diego

  • Become a Patient How to switch your care to UC San Diego Health
  • For Referring Physicians Transfers, referrals and consultations

UC San Diego Health is consistently ranked the No. 1 hospital system in San Diego — and for good reason. Patients across the region choose us for nationally recognized care, leading-edge medicine and a deep commitment to improving lives.

As San Diego's only academic medical center, we deliver seamless care across the full spectrum — from primary care to complex, highly advanced specialty treatment at our specialized hospitals. Here, world-class expertise, innovative research and compassion come together around one goal: you.

Our Mission

To deliver outstanding patient care through a commitment to the community, groundbreaking research and inspired teaching

Our Vision

To create a healthier world — one life at a time — through new science, new medicine and new cures

Best Specialty Care in San Diego

Award-Winning Medical and Surgical Care, Focused on You

When it comes to your health, quality matters. UC San Diego Health is ranked among the nation’s best hospitals by U.S. News & World Report, earning top marks in nine adult specialties, more than any other hospital system in San Diego. The Leapfrog Group gives us "A" grades for hospital safety. These honors reflect the dedication of our physicians, nurses and researchers — and the level of care our patients can expect every day.

Why Is an Academic Medical Center Important?

Care That Goes Beyond Today’s Standard

As the region's only academic medical center, UC San Diego Health doesn't just follow the standard of care — we help define it. The key difference between an academic medical center and a community hospital is integration with a medical school. Many of our physicians are also faculty members at UC San Diego School of Medicine. They conduct original, peer-reviewed research, teach the next generation of doctors and lead advances in medicine. That means your care is guided by experts who are discovering new treatments, leading clinical trials and translating scientific breakthroughs directly to patients. You have access to innovative therapies, advanced technology and clinical trials often before they are available elsewhere — all delivered with personalized, compassionate care.

Top Minds in Modern Medicine

More Than 1,500 Experts Working for You

We are uniquely equipped to deliver world-class patient care, with more than 1,500 physicians and scientists who are leaders in their fields. Our experts publish influential research, pioneer new treatments and help set national standards of care. Among them are 50 current and emeriti members of the prestigious National Academy of Medicine. As a UC San Diego Health patient, you’re choosing care from doctors who aren’t just practicing medicine — they’re saving lives and shaping the future of medicine every day.

Advancing Medicine Through Clinical Trials

Innovation Is in Our DNA, and Part of Everyday Care

For decades, UC San Diego Health — in collaboration with our schools of medicine and pharmacy — has led breakthrough discoveries in surgery, imaging, cancer treatment, cardiovascular care and more. As the only university-based health care system in San Diego focused on developing new medicines and therapies, we offer you access to hundreds of ongoing clinical trials and research studies. Many patients are able to receive promising new treatments here — often before they’re widely available.

Convenient Care Across San Diego

World-Class Facilities Near You

Everything you need is right here — a connected network of brilliant physicians, advanced research technologies and leading-edge care facilities. This includes Jacobs Medical Center and Moores Cancer Center, the region’s only NCI-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center. Our recently opened McGrath Outpatient Pavilion in Hillcrest and Koman Outpatient Pavilion in La Jolla are both modern, welcoming spaces for advanced procedures and convenient pharmacy and lab services. We work with a wide range of health insurance plans and products to help more Californians receive the highest level of care available.

Benefits to the Community in San Diego

As an academic health system, UC San Diego Health proudly serves as a safety net for underserved populations and a partner in community well-being. We are committed to expanding access to care, advancing health education and supporting both physical and mental wellness.
