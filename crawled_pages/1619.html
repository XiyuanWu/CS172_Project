Refer a Patient

Physicians and other clinicians across University of California Health academic health centers look forward to collaborating with you to provide comprehensive, quality care for your patient. 

Our physicians are available for consultations, outpatient referrals and inpatient transfers.  

UC Davis Health Patient Referrals 

Electronic referral form 

Non-urgent outpatient referrals: Call 800-4-UCDAVIS (800-482-3284), option #2 

Urgent referrals: Call 800-4-UCDAVIS (800-482-3284), option #3 

UC San Diego Health Patient Referrals 

Electronic referral form (outpatient) 

Outpatient referrals: Call 855-543-0555 

Inpatient transfer: Call 619-543-5709  

UCI Health Patient Referrals 

Outpatient referrals: Call 877-UCI-DOCS (877-824-3627) 

Inpatient transfer: Call 714-456-2222 

UCLA Health Patient Referrals 

All referrals: Call 1-800-UCLAMD1 (310-825-2631) or email access@mednet.ucla.edu  

Fax referrals: Fax a patient referral to 310-301-5391 

UCR Health Patient Referrals 

All referrals: Call 844-827-8000 

UCSF Health Patient Referrals 

Electronic referral form (pdf)  

Outpatient referrals: Call 800-444-2559 or email referral.center@ucsf.edu 

Inpatient transfer (non-maternity): Call 415-353-9166 

Search Our Clinical Trials   

Across the UCH system, physicians and researchers are running more than 4,600 clinical trials for more than 2,400 conditions. Clinical trials are available across the UCH system, at all of our academic health centers.
