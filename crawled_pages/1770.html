Referrals, Transfers and Consultations

Physician-to-Physician Referral Service

Physician Access Services is a physician-to-physician referral service to expedite inquiries, referrals and transfers from the medical community. We help physicians and their staff access easily patient services and provide information about patient care.

Nursing Program

Care-driven professionals | Magnet® Accredited programs

UC San Diego offers a nationally acclaimed team of nurses who care. Since 2011, we have maintained Magnet® status from the American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC), which is considered among the highest recognitions for nursing excellence and innovation in nursing practice.

Learn About Nursing at UC San Diego Health

UC San Diego Health Affiliated Network

Partnering in an evolving health care landscape

UC San Diego Health Affiliated Network is a collection of providers and health systems serving San Diego, Riverside and Imperial counties. We have come together with a goal to improve patient care and reduce health care costs.

Learn more or join our network

Please note: The current sites included in UC Diego Health's Magnet designation are Hillcrest Medical Center, Jacobs Medical Center, Sulpizio Cardiovascular Center, and UC San Diego Health ambulatory clinic locations. East Campus Medical Center is not designated Magnet at this time.
