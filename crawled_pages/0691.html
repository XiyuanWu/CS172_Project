Campus Tours

Welcome!

We invite you and your family to join us for an in-person walking tour of our beautiful campus located in the heart of California. Our student tour guides are a lively group excited to show you everything they love about UC Merced.

Please ensure you review the weather forecast and adjust your plans accordingly for an enjoyable visit! 

You will also glimpse student life, hear about our academic excellence, student support services, clubs, and organizations, and learn more about internship and research opportunities available to UC Merced students. Sign-up for guided in-person family tours as space is limited.

You can also sign up for self-guided tours or take advantage of the different options for a virtual experience of UC Merced.

We can't wait to meet you!
