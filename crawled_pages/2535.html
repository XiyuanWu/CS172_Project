Tools of the Preservation Trade

Happy Preservation Week! Peek into our Preservation Lab and learn about the tools used to repair and protect the library’s research collections.

Happy 100 Years to the Mother Road!

Celebrate the centennial of America’s most famous highway with a visit to the new Route 66 exhibit at Shields Library, featuring select materials from the Archives and Special Collections Department.

Learn about the Route 66 exhibit
New Student Exhibit Offers Fresh Perspective on the Ancient World

A new exhibit by Classics Department students invites library visitors to examine how the present continues to be shaped and challenged by the past. “The Past in Practice: Interpreting the Ancient World” runs through spring quarter.
