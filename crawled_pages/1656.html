Schools and Programs | UC Davis Health

Empowering Tomorrow’s Health Care Leaders Today

UC Davis schools and education programs offer the highest quality training, skills and values to lead change and improve health for all.

UC Davis School of Medicine

UC Davis School of Medicine is consistently ranked among the nation's leading medical schools across specialties, with top national rankings in primary care and research. Our students gain patient care experience and are trained at UC Davis Medical Center, a premier teaching hospital and the only level 1 trauma center in inland Northern California.

Learn more

Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing at UC Davis

The School of Nursing prepares the next generation of health care leaders through graduate degree programs for aspiring nurses, family nurse practitioners, nurse anesthetists, physician assistants, psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners and nursing science researchers. We advance nursing education, research, clinical practice and health policy to improve care for all communities.

Learn more

Residency and Fellowship Programs

Our nationally recognized residency and fellowship programs and world-class faculty seek to prepare our residents and fellows to excel. Our programs include hands-on patient experience at our nationally recognized UC Davis Medical Center and in our many ambulatory care settings.

See all programs

Education Programs and Training Centers

We have a variety of programs and centers that encompass all aspects of our health care workforce. This includes internships, master’s degree and Ph.D. programs, research and technology programs, and other educational training opportunities.

See more

Professional Development

We encourage our health care community to enhance their skills and continue growing as individuals and leaders. We have a wide array of continuing education options to stay up to date on the latest health care trends and research.
