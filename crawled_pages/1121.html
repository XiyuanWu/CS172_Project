Grad school at UC

Right now you’re probably more concerned about next week’s deadlines than planning the rest of your life. But now is the perfect time to dream about the contribution you’d like to make to the world—and what you’ll need to make it happen. UC is one of the world’s premier research universities and our graduate programs are among the best. You belong here.

With more than 600 graduate degree programs across 10 campuses, the University of California offers a world-class education that can help turn your aspirations into reality. At UC, you'll become part of a dynamic and collaborative community of scholars from across the globe, bringing together a wide range of perspectives and experiences. Together, you’ll pursue knowledge, innovation, and solutions that make a lasting impact on the world.
