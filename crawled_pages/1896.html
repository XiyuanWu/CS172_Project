HEALTH HUMAN RESOURCES

Health Human Resources (HR) is committed to UCI Health’s mission to Discover. Teach. Heal. by empowering people success. Our HR team provides co-workers throughout UCI Health with guidance, information, and support on topics such as talent acquisition, new co-worker onboarding, co-worker training and development, performance appraisals, coaching, and many other services.

Health HR is your strategic partner and trusted advisor in all matters related to the co-worker experience lifecycle. Our services include, but are not limited to, the following. Click on any item to learn more, or contact a Health HRBP or generalist.
