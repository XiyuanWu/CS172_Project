Admissions Overview

 

The SSPPS Office of Admissions and Recruitment (SOAR) and the SSPPS Admissions Committee are committed to recruiting and admitting a talented and diverse cohort of students who demonstrate academic achievement, personal integrity, and a commitment to serving others. We strive to select individuals who will thrive in our PharmD program and go on to become leaders and innovators in the field of pharmacy. The SOAR team can be contacted at sppsadmissions@health.ucsd.edu.

If you are seeking information about the PhD or Master's programs, please note that these programs are overseen by different departments at UC San Diego. Our SOAR team is happy to assist with questions about our PharmD programs, which include the dual PharmD/PhD and 7-Year BS/PharmD programs, but for PhD and Master's program inquiries, please contact the departments directly using the contact information found using the links below:

  • PhD Program in Pharmaceutical Sciences and Drug Development
  • Master's Degree in Drug Development and Product Management
