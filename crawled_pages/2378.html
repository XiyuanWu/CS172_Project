Welcome to UCI COSMOS!

COSMOS (California State Summer School for Mathematics and Science) is a 4-week program for talented high school students hosted by 6 campuses of the University of California. COSMOS students apply to a specific “cluster” taught by esteemed UC faculty, with each cluster addressing advanced topics in science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM) fields, including a variety of engineering disciplines, pharmaceutical sciences, and computer science, to name a few.

Thank you to everyone who applied for COSMOS 2026! If you have been notified that you are on the waitlist, waitlist admissions will continue until the end of May. Next year’s application will be available in January 2027 – stay tuned in December for info sessions!

Thank you for your patience – our website has now been updated! Email cosmos@uci.edu to let us know of any information we may have missed or any formatting errors that disrupt your visit to our site. Please continue to check our Instagram (@ucicosmos) for additional info, like story highlights, input from past students, and more!
