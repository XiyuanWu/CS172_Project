Content & analysis overview

IRAP supports UC’s transparency and advocacy efforts, along with providing decision support through the production of reports and analyses.  This material can include the annual production of the UC Accountability Report, UC Information Center, UC facts at a glance, and UC in California maps which consolidate information across subject areas.  In addition, IRAP produces topic briefs, reports and analyses that concentrate in key areas of the university.

Academic planning

content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with and provides analytical and policy support to the Provost, Academic Planning Council, the UC Academic Senate, the Coordinating Committee on Graduate Affairs and University Committee on Planning and Budget in particular, and all campuses and other parts of UC.

Equity, diversity, and inclusion

Content & analysis section

IRAP partners with UC campuses and UCOP divisions to examine how equity, diversity, and inclusion shape student, faculty, and staff outcomes across the University through data analyses and research collaborations to track progress, surface effective practices, and inform decisions that advance inclusive excellence.

Undergraduate admissions

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with the Undergraduate Admissions office, Diversity and Engagement, and other UCOP departments to provide analytical support to UC administration and the Academic Senate's Board of Admissions & Relations with Schools and provides information to UC campuses to support meeting admissions targets.

Undergraduate enrollment

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with the Undergraduate Deans on campuses and provides decision support, including enrollment modeling, for the enrollment planning process.

Undergraduate affordability

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with the Student and Equity Affairs office and Student Financial Aid in the UC Admissions office to provide data analyses and reports on financial support and program affordability for UC undergraduate students.

Undergraduate outcomes

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with the Undergraduate Education and Institutional Research departments on campuses to assess undergraduate student experience and learning outcomes, and provide decision support on factors influencing graduation rates, trends in degrees awarded, and outcomes.

Graduate and professional degree students

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with the Office of Research and Graduate Studies and campus Graduate Deans to provide analytical and survey support on graduate students.

Alumni economic mobility

content & analysis section

IRAP research illustrates the impact the University of California has on advancing economic mobility for its students, their families, and the state.

Employees

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with UC Systemwide Academic Personnel and Programs on faculty concerns and with UC Systemwide Human Resources on staff concerns.

Research

Content & analysis section

IRAP collaborates with campuses and with various UCOP divisions in analyzing the award and expenditure data to ensure that UC's efforts to secure external support for these critical activities continue to be successful.

Institutional measures

Content & analysis section

IRAP provides data analyses and reports on key metrics in the interest of transparency and accountability.

Public service

Content & analysis section

Public service is the third tine of UC's tripartite mission to provide a world-class education, conduct leading-edge research, and deliver public service, including healthcare, to Californians and the world, while fostering a culture of diversity, equity, and inclusion.
