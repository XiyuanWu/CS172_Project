We steward thriving faculty careers, drive the transformation of the professoriate, and advance the mission of the University of California through an unparalleled portfolio of academic programs.

Faculty Affairs and Academic Programs (FAAP) is home to systemwide academic programs and faculty initiatives that contribute to policy development and management of programs in support of thriving academic careers at the University of California.  We provide faculty and students with opportunities to study and work in Washington, DC, Sacramento and internationally in over 40 countries around the world. Several of our programs offer publication, media, and digital services available to the UC community as well as to California and to the world.
