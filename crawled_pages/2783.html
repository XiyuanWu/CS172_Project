Thank you for considering a philanthropic gift in support of UC Law San Francisco. There are many ways to make your gift to support our students, faculty, and campus community.

Make a Gift Now

Make a one-time, recurring gift, or pledge payment quickly and securely online and support UC Law SF Into the Future.

Give Now

Make checks payable to: UC Law SF Foundation. Please include a note if you would like to designate your gift to a specific fund at the College. Mail a check to our donation lockbox:

Browse giving opportunities in the areas that mean the most to you, including Named & Class Scholarships, Programs & Teams, Student Support, Journals & Faculty Support, Centers & Consortium, Clinical Programs.

Search Now

For generations, UC Law SF has transformed student success into professional success. As donors, you can set a new precedent for student achievement Into the Future.

UC Law San Francisco’s faculty are nationally recognized scholars and dedicated teachers whose influence extends from the classroom to courtrooms and policymaking arenas around the world.

Your support for the Academic Village, another UC Law SF innovative initiative, expands affordable housing for students in one of the nation’s most expensive cities while fostering multidisciplinary collaboration.

Other Ways to Give

Planned Giving

Remembering UC Law San Francisco in your long-range estate plans is one of the most thoughtful and worthwhile decisions you can make. We are grateful for the generous alumni and friends who have the vision to plan for future charitable gifts of any size.

With your support, we are attracting and retaining the brightest students and helping to make UC Law San Francisco’s exceptional legal education more affordable. Learn more here.

Employer Matching Gifts 

Many employers will match your gift to UC Law SF. You may be able to double or triple your contribution. Use the Employer Match tool on our website to check if your employer participates in a matching gift program. Learn more here. 

Gifts of Securities, Stocks, and Bonds 

Appreciated securities, stocks, and bonds can provide significant tax advantages if transferred before being sold. Please consult your tax advisor for guidance. Instructions for transferring stock are available here. 

Wire Transfer 

To contribute by wire transfer, please use the following information:

Cy Pres Funds

UC Law SF’s academic and research centers are eligible recipients for cy pres funds that are generated when courts direct unclaimed or undistributable class action settlement to a nonprofit organization that serves a purpose close to the original intent of the underlying case. Learn more here.

Tax Deductibility and Gift Fee 

Gifts that comply with applicable laws and are made payable to the UC Law SF Foundation (Tax ID: 23-7135898) are tax deductible under Internal Revenue Code Section 501(c)(3).  

A portion of all philanthropic gifts, currently 6%, is allocated to support UC Law SF advancement and alumni relations efforts as a Philanthropic Allocation.

If you have questions about your giving or making a gift, please contact Julia Jordan, Chief Advancement Officer, at jordanjulia@uclawsf.edu or 415.565.4616.
