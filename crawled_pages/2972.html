We provide systemwide financial management, reporting, policies and procedures in support of UC's mission.

UC Controller is part of the University of California, Office of the President (UCOP), the University’s headquarters. We follow our NorthStar principles to demonstrate excellence, innovation, and service as we work toward the University's mission.

As part of the UC Finance division, the office provides systemwide financial management, reporting, policies and procedures. Our services include:

  • Efficient and effective financial service delivery
  • Best practices and guidance to campuses, medical centers and foundations
  • Development and administration of systemwide policies and procedures
  • Compliance with university, state and federal laws and regulations
