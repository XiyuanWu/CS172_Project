Office Information

UCR Health - Riverside Downtown (Primary Care) location is conveniently located at Citrus Tower in the heart of downtown Riverside. Services include Primary Care (Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, and Pediatrics).

Located inside the Citrus Tower building on the first floor.

Parking Info: Starting February 1, 2024, UCR Health - Riverside Downtown (Primary Care) and UCR Health - Riverside Downtown (Specialty Care) at Citrus Tower Corporate Plaza will no longer offer parking validations for patients using the underground garage, subject to building rates. Please visit www.ucrhealth.org/citrustowerparking/ for more information.

Preparing for your Appointment

  • A current list of any medications you take, including over-the-counter medications, vitamin supplements and herbal products
  • Your insurance card and co-payment, if required
  • A deposit if you will be paying for the visit
  • Doctor's referral, if required
  • Recent test results related to your condition
  • List of questions you may have
  • Device or paper for taking notes
