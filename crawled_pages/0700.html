2020 LRDP

The University has prepared a draft Subsequent Environmental Impact Report (SEIR) that evaluates the potentially significant environmental effects of the proposed 2020 LRDP. Both the 2020 LRDP and SEIR documents are available at planning.ucmerced.edu/2020LRDP. If you would like to submit comments or have any questions regarding the 2020 LRDP and SEIR documents, please contact us at 2020LRDP@ucmerced.edu.

Our Vision Statement

To create a global model for university development and physical operations that excels in safety, customer service, sustainability and teamwork through innovation. All aspects of this model advance the university’s mission of teaching, research, and public service. Physical Operations, Planning and Development (POPD) implements an organizational structure that is cohesive, efficient and promotes a coordinated approach to the physical campus environment.
