Improving Health for All

Research

We believe improving health for all is possible. So, our collaborative research includes clinical, translational and basic science studies.

Trailblazing Innovations

At any given moment, research is underway in every corner of our organization. Much of our research is aimed at developing technologies, treatments and therapies that directly improve the lives of our patients.

Clinical Trials

At the core of our mission is to discover and share knowledge and find breakthroughs that address health challenges. We offer more clinical trials than any other health care provider in our region.

Research Centers and Programs

We offer some of the nation’s most prominent research centers and programs, led by nationally and internationally renowned faculty and research teams.

Explore research programs

Comprehensive Cancer Center

Our researchers tackle some of the most complex challenges in cancer prevention, diagnosis and treatment. We're also part of the University of California’s cancer research network, harnessing the power of the nation’s largest cancer research program to find treatments for the most difficult cases.

Learn morearrow_forward

Center for Healthcare Policy and Research

We facilitate research to improve public health by contributing new knowledge about access, delivery, cost, quality, and outcomes related to health care.

Learn morearrow_forward

Institute for Regenerative Cures

The Institute for Regenerative Cures’ Stem Cell Program brings together world-class physicians, research scientists, biomedical engineers, and other experts and collaborators. We help develop precision medicine therapies, which are personalized to you and your condition.

Learn morearrow_forward

MIND Institute

Both a research and treatment center, the MIND Institute conducts collaborative research in search of cures and treatments for neurodevelopmental conditions.

Learn morearrow_forward

Research at the Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing

Research at the School of Nursing is the engine that drives new solutions to put individuals, as well as families and communities, at the center of care.

Learn morearrow_forward

Research at the School of Medicine

Recognized among the nation’s top schools for research, our collaborative culture and innovative partnerships are transforming health care and improving lives.

Researcher Resources

Clinical and Translational Science Center (CTSC)

CTSC partners with UC Davis schools and colleges engaged in life science and translational research. Our goal is to support collaborative projects with the potential to improve health care.

Learn morearrow_forward

School of Medicine Office of Research

The Office of Research supports investigators who perform biomedical research at UC Davis Health. We manage research awards and proposals and oversee laboratory safety and research space, among other services.

Learn morearrow_forward

Clinical Trials Office

The Clinical Trials Office assists investigators, scholars, and staff in the conduct of clinical research. We provide education in regulatory and clinical research administration, help with study recruitment, and other supportive resources.

Learn morearrow_forward

Training and Career Development

Translational research training offers scholars a rich array of career development opportunities through program curricula, mentored research training, and partnerships with other programs, departments and institutions.

Learn morearrow_forward

Human Subjects Research – IRB

The Institutional Review Board (IRB) at UC Davis is responsible for oversight of research involving human participants. We provide information about submitting a project for IRB Review and other resources, such as participant rights and training.

Learn morearrow_forward

Grants Facilitation

The Grants Facilitation Unit at the School of Medicine Office of Research assists investigators across the research career spectrum with grant proposals to numerous sponsors. We offer one-on-one consultations, proposal review, educational seminars and more.

Learn morearrow_forward
