Cancer Services & Treatment

Receive Oncology Care at a Top Cancer Center of America

At UCLA Health, teams of cancer specialists work together to treat and assist cancer patients and their families. This multidisciplinary approach to oncology care helps patients and family members confront both the immediate and long-term effects of cancer.

The UCLA Health Jonsson Comprehensive Cancer Center has been designated a "comprehensive cancer center" by the National Cancer Institute, a testament to its excellence in cancer research and education, and its strong partnership with UCLA Health to provide outstanding patient care and community outreach in cancer prevention. Our physicians are global leaders in the diagnosis and treatment of numerous types of cancer, and our hospitals are consistently ranked among the best in the nation by U.S. News & World Report.

Why Choose Oncology Care at UCLA Health?

A comprehensive cancer center

Patients benefit from a full range of oncology care and support services from doctors, clinics, centers and hospitals crossing more than 40 scientific and medical disciplines. UCLA Health is consistently ranked as one of the best cancer centers in California, and among the top in the United States, by U.S. News & World Report.

The most advanced cancer treatment

The Cancer Center has established an international reputation for developing new cancer therapies, providing the best in experimental and traditional oncology treatments. Successful targeted therapies such as Herceptin®, Gleevec, and Sprycel were developed based on basic science performed in UCLA laboratories and clinical trials with UCLA Health patients.

An interdisciplinary approach

Patients receive care from teams of specialists — including oncologists, surgeons, pathologists, radiologists, nurses, genetic counselors and more — who collaborate to design a treatment plan tailored to each individual. This team-based, multidisciplinary approach ensures that every aspect of care is coordinated and connected.

Personalized guidance and support

Our dedicated nurse navigators help patients understand their diagnosis, coordinate appointments, access resources, and manage side effects — ensuring you never have to navigate the cancer journey alone.

More than treatment

In addition to leading-edge therapies, patients have access to comprehensive services including early detection, prevention, integrative oncology, wellness programs, nutrition counseling, psychological support and survivorship care.

Survivorship programs

Programs such as the Breast Cancer Wellness Program offer long-term, specialized follow-up care focused on health monitoring, symptom management, wellness promotion and quality of life — ensuring survivors feel supported well beyond active treatment.

Collaboration that drives discovery

Close collaboration between researchers and physicians leads to scientific discoveries and improved cancer detection, prevention and treatment for patients.

Many locations

Clinics, centers and hospitals are located in Westwood, Santa Monica, throughout Southern California and the Central Coast. Clinical trials are offered across California and the United States.
