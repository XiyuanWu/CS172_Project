Welcome to UCR Student Health Services

The mission of the Student Health Services (SHS) is to promote academic excellence, enrich the student experience, and support retention by providing high-quality, accessible, and comprehensive medical care to students with a focus on multidisciplinary services, health education, and prevention.

News and Announcements

Immunization Verification

All incoming UCR students must complete immunization verification. Avoid a Registration Hold. Visit the How to Submit UC Immunization Requirements page to complete the process.

 
SHS Update: Policy on External Provider Orders

Regarding lab tests and other diagnostic services (including radiology, X-ray, and ultrasound) ordered by external providers:  UCR Student Health Service (SHS) has a new policy can only process orders from SHS healthcare providers. 

Here's what this means:

  • If you have a lab or diagnostic order from a doctor or clinic outside of SHS, we cannot directly accept it at the SHS lab or imaging facilities.
  • Find an in-network lab or diagnostic center if you're enrolled in UC SHIP (use the Sydney app).

Questions? Contact us at 951-827-3031 (option 2) or the patient portal. We're here to help!

 

Online Nurse Scheduling

You can conveniently schedule appointments with our nurses online through UCR Student Health. Whether you're a member of UC SHIP, GSHIP, or Highlander Care, our team is ready to assist you. Use our online portal to book appointments or call us at (951) 827-3031 option #2 for services including:

  • Acute triage: Receive initial assessment for any non-emergency health concern.
  • Medication questions: Consult with our nurses regarding your medications.
  • Urinary tract infection (UTI) screening: Get screened for UTI and discuss treatment options.
  • Other miscellaneous acute care needs: We provide care for various non-emergency health concerns.

Additionally, nurse appointments include contraception services such as emergency contraception and oral contraceptive pill refills.

Pharmacy
  1. The SHS Pharmacy is open and available for prescription pick-ups, drop-offs, and refills.
  2. OTC medications are available for purchase without a prescription including emergency contraception.
  3. Please call (951) 827-3926 if you have any questions regarding Pharmacy Services.

Family Planning and Abortion Care

1. Call (951) 827-3031 and ask for Family Planning and Abortion Care Service.

2. Patient will have an appointment with the on-campus nurse at Student Health Services.

3. Student Health Services sends a referral to FPA Women's health Telehealth team.

4. Medications will be mailed directly to the patient for secure delivery.

5. Virtual follow ups are scheduled after one week and two weeks to ensure the effectiveness of the procedure.

Student Surveys
  1.  Please take a few minutes to complete the online Student Health Services Satisfaction Survey about your most recent visit. All information will be kept confidential and used solely for quality assurance and improvement.

Health Records Information

 

  • Current/Previous Students
    Current/Previous Students
    • For release of information, please log into your UCR patient portal  to complete your request.
    • Review the release of information instructions for more details on submitting your request.
    • For all other requests requiring a signature, please download and complete the Release of Information Authorization Form.
    Health Records Submission Information

    Submit your completed forms by fax, mail, or email to:

    University of California, Riverside

    Student Health and Counseling Center, Student Health Services, Medical Records 
    388 W. Linden Street
    Riverside, CA 92521
    Fax: (833) 260-8603
    Email:     shsmedrecords@ucr.edu

    Please allow seven to 10 business days for processing.

    No fee is charged for requests that are sent directly to a physician’s office, medical facility, hospital, medical insurance company, school, State of California disability office, Social Security office, or law enforcement office.

    Fees for Student Personal Requests

    Please determine on your request how you would like to receive your records.

    • Electronic processing: Flat fee of $6.50. Records can be faxed, emailed encrypted with a password, or copied to a CD for mailing or pick up.
    • Paper: 25 cents per paper page copied. Please choose only records that you need to lower your costs if you require paper records.

    A flat fee of $15 is charged for third-party requests (ex., copy services, attorneys, auto insurance etc.).

    Payments

    Current or former students: You will receive a secure encrypted invoice by email if an email address is provided or by mail with your records. We cannot accept cash. Checks or money orders are to be made out to UC Regents and must include your student ID number (if applicable). Checks or money orders can be mailed or dropped off with a copy of the invoice enclosed to:

    University of California, Riverside
    Student Health and Counseling Center, Student Health Services, Medical Records 
    388 W. Linden Street 
    Riverside, CA 92521

    Third parties: Please attach a $15 check made out to UC Regents with each request in order to process.

Confidentiality

UCR is committed to patient confidentiality. We safeguard your medical records according to established professional practices.

UCR handles your health information based on the following: 

  • If you are a student

    If you are a student*, the University of California follows the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA)  the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and other applicable laws that apply to the use and disclosure of your health information. 

    *Whether you are a student or not is defined by Section 130.00 of the University of California’s Policies Applying to Campus Activities, Organizations and Students (PACAOS).

  • If you are a non-student

    If you are a non-student, we may use and disclose your health information as described in the University of California Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Notice of Privacy Practices.
