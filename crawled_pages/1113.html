Our veterans coordinators can help you access and make the most of your veterans’ benefits, and connect you with campus services.

Contact a campus veterans coordinator

Programs and services

You’ll find numerous programs at our nine undergraduate campuses:

  • Priority registration for classes
  • Veteran-specific campus orientation
  • Academic advising and career counseling
  • Financial services such as a deferred payment plan, short-term loans, and campus VA work study
  • Pre and post-deployment support
  • Veteran housing services and student centers
  • Student veteran organizations

You have what it takes

The University of California – one of the best public university systems in the world – has a long history of welcoming student veterans and military personnel to our campuses. Whether you’re a current or former member of the military, you’ll find a supportive community dedicated to helping you succeed.

Student veteran facts

  • Most of UC's veterans who enroll as undergraduates started at a California Community College.
  • UC undergraduate veterans receive substantial grants and scholarships, in addition to VA education benefits for which many student veterans are eligible.
  • More than 2,000 veterans and students with military affiliation attend UC.
