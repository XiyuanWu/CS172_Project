The Susan & Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences

The Susan & Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences

The UC Irvine Susan & Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences (COHS) prepares the diverse healthcare workforce of the future and drives groundbreaking scientific discovery in close partnership with UCI Health, Orange County’s premier academic health system. Together, the college and health system integrate education, research and patient care to improve lives across the region, state and nation, guided by our mission: Discover. Teach. Heal.

About the Samueli Gift
School of Medicine
Dean Michael J. Stamos, MD (Since 2017)
Ranked as one of the top 50 U.S. medical schools for research, the UC Irvine School of Medicine advances medical knowledge and clinical practice through scholarly research, physician education and high-quality patient care. As part of a unified academic health system, the school prepares medical students, residents and physician-scientists to lead in both clinical excellence and scientific discovery, while translating research advances into meaningful improvements in health.
Sue & Bill Gross School of Nursing
Dean Mark Lazenby, PhD, APRN, FAAN (Since 2021)
The Sue & Bill Gross School of Nursing educates nursing professionals who improve patient care and promote population health, locally and globally. The school prepares leaders in research, education and clinical practice who champion collaborative, compassionate and interdisciplinary, evidence-based care.
School of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences
Dean Jan Hirsch, BS Pharm, PhD (Since 2019)
The School of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences offers undergraduate and graduate students unparalleled training for future careers in pharmacy, medicine and biomedical research. Its innovative, rigorous curriculum integrates concepts from biology, chemistry, chemical engineering, pharmacology and physiology. Alumni pursue professional opportunities that improve the health and well-being of communities locally and beyond.
Joe C. Wen School of Population & Public Health
Dean Bernadette Boden-Albala, MPH, DrPH (Since 2019)
Wen Public Health creates, integrates and disseminates population and community-based knowledge to promote health and reduce the societal burden of disease and disability. Through excellence in research, education and public service, the school advances health equity and prepares leaders who address complex public health challenges.
Susan Samueli Integrative Health Institute
Associate Vice Chancellor and Executive Director Shaista Malik, MD, PhD, MPH (Since 2017)
The Samueli Institute advances scientific research, education and clinical care that integrates conventional and complementary medicine. Its vision is to be the preeminent national and international academic institute for pioneering multidisciplinary research, education and health care practices for the care of the whole person.
Henry Samueli, 2019
“We are very excited for the UCI College of Health Sciences to become a national model for integrative health. We believe this model will eventually become the standard approach for promoting health and well-being in our society.”

Historical Timeline

Much has changed from the early days when the medical school came to campus and a county hospital was acquired to the creation of the Susan & Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences and the evolution of UC Irvine’s academic health system. What remains constant is our dedication to educating future healthcare leaders, conducting breakthrough research and delivering compassionate, evidence-based care that improves lives.
