Explore Undergraduate Education

At UC San Diego, academic excellence and innovation are at the core of the undergraduate experience. With a wide breadth of majors and minors, along with diverse academic opportunities, students have the tools to discover their own path and achieve their educational goals. Explore the variety of academic offerings and the guidance available to support students.

Learn about University Requirements

Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

All undergraduate students are required to take at least one Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) course during their studies. Learn more about the course offerings and the origins of this important requirement on our campus.

Learn More

Jane Teranes Climate Change Education

All undergraduate students are required to complete at least one Jane Teranes Climate Change Education Requirement (JTCCER) course during their studies. Explore the course offerings and discover how this requirement came to be a part of UC San Diego’s commitment to climate education.

Learn More

Undergraduate Seminars

UC San Diego's Undergraduate Seminar Programs are offered during the Fall, Winter, and Spring quarters, serving over 1,500 students annually. These programs include First-Year Seminars, Senior Seminars, and Research Seminars. Explore this site for more information about the seminars and guidelines on submitting seminar proposals.
