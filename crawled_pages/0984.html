Connect with Student Organizations

Build your community and skills through involvement

At UC Santa Cruz, students don’t just join organizations—they lead them. Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) are student-initiated, student-led organizations recognized by the University of California and supported through SOMeCA: Student Organization Advising & Resources (SOAR), Student Media, and Cultural Arts & Diversity (CAD).

Rooted in the Student Agency Model, RSOs offer real-world experience in leadership, collaboration, budgeting, advocacy, and event planning. They’re also one of the most powerful ways to build community, connect with peers, and create a sense of belonging on campus. With guidance from dedicated SOMeCA advisors, students build skills that prepare them for life now and beyond college—academically, professionally, and personally.

Whether you’re ready to join an organization, start your own, plan an event, or apply for funding, this page has everything you need to get started. Wherever you are on your student org journey, we’re here to support you.

Interested in joining a student organization? Email soar@ucsc.edu to get connected.
