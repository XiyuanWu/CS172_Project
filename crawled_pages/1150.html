Emergency Services for UCLA Students

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) offers a variety of services to meet students needs including:

  • Crisis counseling available by phone 24 hours a day/7 days a week
  • Emergency intervention
  • Individual counseling and psychotherapy
  • Group therapy
  • Psychiatric evaluation and treatment
  • Psychoeducational programs and workshops for students, staff and faculty
  • Campus mental health and wellness promotion

Rape Treatment Center

Victims can receive highly specialized emergency medical care, forensic services, counseling, advocacy, and information about their rights and options to support them in making informed choices and decisions 24 hours a day. If you or someone you know has been sexually assaulted or sexually abused, call us.

National Suicide Prevention Lifeline

Languages: English, Spanish
Phone: 1-800-273-TALK

National Domestic Violence Prevention Hotline

Languages: English, Spanish
Phone: 1-800-799-SAFE

National Sexual Assault Hotline

Languages: English, Spanish
Phone: 1-800-656-HOPE
Online chat: Visit online.rainn.org to chat one-on-one with a trained RAINN support specialist.
Mobile app: Get support, information, advice, or a referral anywhere, anytime from your smartphone with the RAINN mobile app. Download it on the Apple App Store or the Google Play store.
