New Podcasting Studio at Science Library

Ready to record your next big idea? The Podcasting Studio in the Multimedia Resources Center makes it easy to create clean, professional sounding audio with high quality gear like Shure SM7B mics and Sony studio headphones. Make a reservation and start creating today!

Exhibit | Comics Beyond the Margins

Explore comics like you've never seen before! 

From heartbreaking memoirs to surreal, intergalactic tales, Comics Beyond the Margins features comics from UC Irvine Libraries’ general collections, 1960s to the present. With humor and stunning graphics, the exhibit covers wide-ranging themes and diverse modes of storytelling, illustrating the current realities of our world and what a better future might look like for all. 

On display in Langson Library from March through September during regular library hours/
