UC Davis Medical Center

Welcome to UC Davis Medical Center. Our nationally recognized academic medical center offers exceptional care for you and your family in the following areas:

  • Primary care for all ages and specialty care in 150 fields
  • The latest treatment options and expertise for the most complex health conditions
  • A recognized commitment to diversity, inclusion and culturally sensitive care
