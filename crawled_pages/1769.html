Health and Medicine

UC San Diego Health is an internationally recognized academic health system that provides compassionate, personalized patient care alongside leading-edge bench-to-bedside research. Your support enables our physician-scientists to investigate therapeutic options while educating the medical leaders of tomorrow.

Do you already know which fund you would like to support? Search for a fund and make your gift here.

Featured Funds

Future of Care

UC San Diego Health is creating a healthier world, one life at a time, through award-winning academic medicine. Your investment in the UC San Diego Health Fund moves us closer to redefining how we diagnose, treat and cure disease in our community, our nation and our world.

GIVE NOW

Grow with Us

UC San Diego Health continues to evolve with the expansion of clinical care facilities at our La Jolla and Hillcrest campuses and other locations throughout our region. But these are more than just buildings — they’re solutions to our problems, investments in our future and promises to our community. With each new structure, our academic medical system strengthens its tripartite mission of excellence in education, research and providing world-class patient care.

Other Giving Priorities

Cancer Center

Gifts to the Research Fund at Moores Cancer Center are directed toward research projects that will enable us to deliver new discoveries more quickly.

Cardiovascular Institute

As part of the only academic health system in San Diego, the Sulpizio Cardiovascular Center is a hub of discovery and innovation. Your gift supports the Division of Cardiolgy with flexible funding that helps move the latest technologies, treatments and techniques in cardiac care from bench to bedside.

Give Now

Center for Healthy Aging

As we live longer healthier lives, the Center for Healthy Aging is applying new advances to improve our quality of life. Your gift enhances our efforts to prevent and reduce the burden of disability and disease in late life through innovative research and public education efforts.

Give Now

Center for Psychedelic Research

Understanding how humanity can benefit from psychedelics and mind-body approaches is the next frontier of medical research. Your support will accelerate our vision to quantify the psychological, physiological and neural effects of psychedelics; assess the medical value of these treatments; and investigate the unique states of consciousness these compounds induce.

UC San Diego Health Family Houses

Gifts support the Bannister Family House and the La Jolla Family House and their efforts to provide a haven of hope and comfort for families with a loved one in long-term or critical care at UC San Diego Health.

Give Now

Mother Milk Infant Center of Research Excellence

Support for the Mother Milk Infant Center of Research Excellence fuels discoveries that allow us to better understand the composition of human milk.

Give Now

Psychiatry - Eating Disorders

Support for UC San Diego’s eating disorder research empowers us to examine the neurobiological roots of eating disorders using the latest technologies in neuroimaging and genetics to develop data-driven treatments.

Shiley Eye Institute

From compassionate clinical care to leading-edge research, support for Shiley Eye Institute at UC San Diego Health advances efforts to prevent and cure blindness here in San Diego and far beyond.

Stem Cell Research

UC San Diego plays a leading role in stem cell research, pioneering our understanding of the foundations of human development and disease. Your gift supports stem cell research in pursuit of cures and therapies for a wide range of diseases and disorders.

Student-Run Free Clinic

The Student-Run Free Clinic provides compassionate care to San Diego’s underserved communities while offering valuable experiential learning opportunities for UC San Diego medical students. Your gift provides flexible funding to help us meet a critical community need while nurturing the next generation of health professionals.

Women's Health
