Our Strategic Direction and the People Who Drive It

At the UCSF School of Medicine, we are focused on the future – shaped by our shared commitment to advancing health worldwide through excellence in education, research, clinical care, and public service. Our strategic plan charts a bold course to support the people who make this institution extraordinary: our faculty, staff, learners, and community partners.


Together, we are building an academic health system that drives scientific discovery, reimagines education, advances health equity, and fosters a culture of belonging and well-being. We are investing in leadership development, expanding access to career pathways, and strengthening connections across our missions.


This work is both aspirational and urgent. Our progress depends on cultivating the talents of our people, listening with humility, and aligning our culture with our values. Every member of our community plays a vital role in shaping the future of UCSF – and the future of academic medicine.

Dr. Talmadge E. King, Jr.

Dean, UCSF School of Medicine
