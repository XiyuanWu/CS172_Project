Welcome to San Francisco’s Academic Village

San Francisco’s Academic Village is a pioneering shared campus — designed to provide academic facilities and campus housing for higher education institutions with existing or desired programs in San Francisco. By joining the Academic Village, an institution of higher education receives the academic or residential spaces they lease/use/occupy, along with access to the entirety of the campus , complete with all the amenities desired by today’s students and professionals.

Partner with the Academic Village to access campus housing, programmatic space, and a multidiscipline campus in San Francisco. 

Partner with us

Learning, living, and community on a shared campus in the heart of San Francisco.

Academic Village supports San Francisco’s academic collaborations among educational institutions. The Academic Village also connects community partners by leveraging its central location in San Francisco’s Civic Center, Mid-Market, and Tenderloin neighborhoods. The Academic Village emphasizes multidisciplinary teaching and interdisciplinary engagement.
