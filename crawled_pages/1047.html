The Student Success Center is located at the Parnassus campus in Millberry Union West. This is a great resource for students, here you can find Basic Needs, First Generation Services, Guardian Scholars, Learning Resource Services, Registered Campus Organizations, Student Disability Services, Student Government, Student Rights and Responsibilities, Synapse (the student run newspaper), and Student Veteran and Military Support Services. We can also help connect you to other campus resources and services.

Other things you’ll find at the Student Success Center:

  • Food market
  • Snack Cart (free menstrual products offered)
  • The Pantry
  • Photo booth (professional headshots)
  • Reserve meeting/study space: The SSC Alumni Room is a quiet space for students to study, hold a meeting or RCO group, and can accommodate from 1 to 9 students. To reserve, please email [email protected]. (Available to reserve Monday  Fri. 9 a.m. - 4 p.m.)
