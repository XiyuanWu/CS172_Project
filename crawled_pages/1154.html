Student Health and Well-Being

Student Health and Well-Being (SHW) is a one-stop destination dedicated to supporting students’ physical and mental health throughout their academic journey. The cluster takes a holistic approach to well-being, offering high-quality medical care, proactive health education and compassionate mental health support.

Student Health and Well-Being

Student Health and Well-Being cluster’s mission is to cultivate a campus community that fosters life-long, holistic health and well-being through collaboration, and where everyone can thrive and flourish in a diverse, inclusive and supportive environment. 

EXPLORE SHW RESOURCES

Student Health Services

Student Health Services (SHS) offers a comprehensive range of quality medical services — conveniently located right on-campus — tailored to meet the diverse needs of our student community.

Learn more about Student Health Services

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) takes a student-centered approach to comprehensive mental health support, offering counseling, workshops, outreach programs and more to help students flourish during their time on campus and beyond.

Find out more about CAPS

Health Promotion Services

Health Promotion Services (HPS) empowers students to develop healthy habits through education, events, supportive services and resources and cultivate lifelong health and well-being.

Visit Health Promotion Services

Student Health Insurance

UC Student Health Insurance Plan (UC SHIP) is a high quality, affordable health plan designed for undergraduate and graduate students that provides comprehensive coverage, including medical, mental health, dental, pharmacy and vision.

Visit UC SHIP and Raft
