President James B. Milliken

“Now more than ever, the future of our state, our country, and our world depends on thriving, innovative, and accessible universities." – President Milliken

James B. Milliken is the 22nd president of the University of California. He oversees UC's world-renowned university system of 10 campuses, six academic health centers, three affiliated national laboratories, more than 290,000 students and 230,000 faculty and staff.
