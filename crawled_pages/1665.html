Telehealth Services

UC Davis Health offers patients and families several telehealth options designed to keep you connected to your health and the health care needs of your loved ones.

You can use the MyUCDavisHealth app or web portal to manage your health and schedule telehealth video visits for certain types of appointments. Explore the services below to learn more.

Getting Patient Care

  • Video Visits

    The MyUCDavisHealth app or web portal brings your care team to you, allowing you to have a medical appointment from the comfort of your own home using your smartphone, tablet or personal computer.

    Video visits offer convenience for:

    • Pre- and post-procedure care
    • Mental health follow-ups and behavioral related services
    • Diabetes management, pharmacy consultations and reconciliations
    • Oncology follow-ups

    Talk to your health care team to find out if telehealth video visits are an option for you or your family member.

    Learn more about telehealth video visitsarrow_forward

  • Telehealth Express Care

    Accessing care has never been easier. Connect with UC Davis Health care teams using our online Express Care option for same-day and extended-hours video visits to discuss non-emergency issues such as:

    • Flu-like symptoms
    • Urinary tract issues
    • GI problems
    • Joint pain and more

    Express Care is available through the MyUCDavisHealth app or web portal.

    Learn more about telehealth express carearrow_forward
Specialty care for rural and urban areas

Provider-to-Provider Telehealth

Rural and urban communities throughout Northern California benefit from our innovative telehealth program. Patients don’t have to leave their community to gain access to UC Davis Health specialists located hundreds of miles away. Our telehealth consultations are a collaborative meeting between the primary care provider, specialist and patient.

Stay connected to your health

MyUCDavisHealth

As a UC Davis Health patient, you can use the MyUCDavisHealth app or online web portal to securely and confidentially communicate with your care team, review your medical record, and much more – all from a smartphone, tablet or computer.

MyUCDavisHealth gives you free, secure and confidential access to:

  • Schedule appointments and receive personalized reminders
  • Send and receive non-urgent messages from your care team
  • Review your medications, immunizations, allergies, and medical history
  • Request medication refills, and view lab and test results
  • View after-visit summaries, discharge information, and more
Learn more about MyUCDavisHealth
Connecting Families During Hospital Stays

  • Family Video Visits

    To keep families connected to their loved one’s health and healing during their hospital stay, family members can now join in on video visits with the patient and/or the patient’s care team.

    Learn more about family video visitsarrow_forward

  • NICU Webcam Program

    The webcam program equips every bed at the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU) with a webcam. This allows parents and families to see their baby through a secure connection from a home computer, tablet or cellphone 24/7, even when they are not at the bedside.

    Learn more about the webcam programarrow_forward
