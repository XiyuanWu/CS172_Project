Free Expression at UC San Diego

Free expression is a cornerstone of the U.S. Constitution. UC San Diego supports student expression, rights and responsibilities to move toward positive, educational use of Free Speech.

Safety Concerns

  • Students, staff, and faculty who choose to participate in free expressive activity and are concerned for the immediate safety of themselves or others should call or text 9-1-1 and indicate their location on campus.

  • If you believe that you have experienced harassment or acts of discrimination during an expressive activity, please contact the Office for the Prevention of Harassment and Discrimination (OPHD).

  • If you are unsure how to address your experience but would like to speak with someone, please contact the Dean of Student Affairs in your Undergraduate College or Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs (GEPA).
