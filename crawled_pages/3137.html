Notice of Regents Meeting, May 5-6, 2026

The Regents of the University of California and its committees will meet on May 5-6, 2026 at UCLA. 

Click on each Committee/Board meeting below to see the agendas and on each open session agenda item to see background materials. Please note that all times indicated and the order of business are approximate and subject to change.

The wearing of masks or any face covering in the meeting room is prohibited, except that an attendee may wear a single, transparent mask provided at the meeting entrance.* Individuals wearing non-transparent masks or face coverings may view the meeting from the designated Overflow Room. 

*The mask provided at the meeting entrance must be worn in the manner intended by the manufacturer.

Live video streaming is available during the open session meetings.
