Office of Communications

The Office of Communications informs and empowers the public with stories about UCSF’s mission to advance health worldwide. We collaborate with researchers, educators, clinicians and other health care professionals to promote the discoveries and innovations of one of the world’s leading health sciences universities.

Resources

Find guidelines for all your communication needs, contact our office, and join the UCSF Communicators Network for opportunities to connect with other UCSF communicators.
