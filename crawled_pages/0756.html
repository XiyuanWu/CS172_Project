Donor-Advised Funds

Reach your philanthropic goals and make a meaningful impact with a donor-advised fund (DAF).

  • What is a donor-advised fund (DAF)?

    A donor-advised fund (DAF) is a popular, tax-efficient way to support the organizations you care about through a charitable giving account. This account allows you to invest funds and then recommend grants to your chosen organizations and causes in minutes through easy to use avenues like DAFpay. When you contribute money to your DAF, you’ll receive a tax deduction.

  • How do donor-advised funds work?

    A donor-advised fund can be set up through your financial institution or community foundation. Examples include Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, and BNY Mellon. Your contributions will be invested, and you can then recommend grants to distribute your funds as you wish. These grants may be one-time gifts or recurring donations over time.

    To learn more about establishing a DAF, contact your community foundation or financial advisor.

    Once established, it is easier than ever to direct your grants to institutions like UC Riverside through DAFpay, now available through our online giving forms.

  • What are the benefits of a donor-advised fund (DAF)?

    DAFs allow you as the donor to:

    • Contribute a wide range of assets to a single philanthropic fund.
    • Receive a tax deduction on your contributions. Please note that grants made through a donor-advised fund are not eligible for additional charitable tax deductions, as the deduction is taken from the original contribution.
    • Collaborate with your DAF sponsor to find organizations that align with your values.
    • Manage administrative tasks, such as recordkeeping and disbursement, in one location.
    • Invest donations for tax-free growth.
    • Support your legacy through planned giving - you can arrange with your DAF sponsor to bequeath your remaining funds to your favorite charity or nonprofit.
       

Give via DAFpay today via our online giving forms or contact us at (951) 827-5611 to learn more.
