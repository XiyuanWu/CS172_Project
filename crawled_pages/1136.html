What is UROP?

The Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) provides advising, resources, and events to help UCI undergraduate students from any major get involved in research and creative activities with UCI faculty.

Learn about UROP

How We Help You

FIND research opportunities and creative activities

Our workshops and information sessions teach you how to connect with faculty mentors and apply to research positions.

APPLY to UROP Opportunities

Earn recognition and possible funding for your UCI faculty-mentored project.

SHOWCASE your work

Showcase your research at the UC Irvine Undergraduate Research Symposium or Project Pitch Challenge.

Why Do Research?

  • Explore your interests and gain valuable hands-on experience
  • Develop your skills in communication, critical thinking, and professionalism
  • Build mentoring relationships with UCI faculty, researchers, and your peers

Why Become a UROP Fellow?

UC Irvine undergraduate students may apply for a UROP award to receive recognition of their faculty-mentored research or creative activity. Upon selection, they become UROP Fellows and present at the UC Irvine Undergraduate Research Symposium.



Earn recognition that will make you stand out on your resume and in graduate school applications



Qualify to apply to funding opportunities



Get support, resources, and guidance from UROP staff to ensure your success!
