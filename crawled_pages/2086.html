Building Better Qubits

We are accelerating the discovery and optimization of quantum materials, paving the way for faster, more reliable quantum computers.

Advancing Quantum Computing

We are advancing hardware, algorithms, software, and control systems to make practical quantum computing a reality.

We transform fundamental breakthroughs into the building blocks of next-generation quantum devices, partnering with industry to create technologies that will tackle challenges not possible today and drive the future of computing, national security, and scientific discovery.

Berkeley Lab offers technologies for licensing in quantum computing hardware, software, and control systems and quantum materials innovations.

Berkeley Lab is driving quantum innovation by integrating theory, hardware, and networking to accelerate discovery and deliver secure, scalable quantum technologies for the nation.

From microelectronics to quantum computing, biomanufacturing, and more, our research powers economic growth and progress across the nation. Explore our impact state-by-state — from business partnerships to technology licenses and more.
