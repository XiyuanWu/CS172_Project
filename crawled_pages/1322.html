Summit Meetings

University of California Chicanx Latinx Alumni Association (UC CLAA) summits provide a unique opportunity to connect with Chicanx Latinx alumni from all ten UC campuses; stay updated on the latest alumni and UC developments in areas such as binational entrepreneurship, climate change, health equity, and alumni engagement; and explore opportunities for new or expanded binational collaborations.
