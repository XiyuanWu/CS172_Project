UC IRVINE ALUMNI ASSOCIATION

Derek Sabori, ’95, MBA ’02, Named Executive Director of UC Irvine Alumni Association

Derek Sabori, ’95, MBA ’02, has been named Executive Director of the UC Irvine Alumni Association, marking an exciting new chapter for the UC Irvine Alumni Association (UCIAA) and the university’s growing global alumni community. A proud alum and past president of the UC Irvine Alumni Association Board, Derek brings deep alumni perspective and a passion for connection as UC Irvine continues its remarkable momentum.

Vice Chancellor Brian Hervey shared that Derek “understands UC Irvine, our alumni, and has the vision and experience to build on this extraordinary moment.” Derek added, “I am deeply honored to step into this role at a university that fundamentally shaped my life and career.” Read more in the press release and join us in celebrating the future of the Anteater alumni community.
