Application Process

01

Determine your student type. (See "Students" drop down menu.)

02

Explore courses at classes.berkeley.edu.

03

Beginning in February, apply to Summer Sessions.

04

Follow email instructions to enroll.

“The main reason why I chose to participate in Summer Sessions at UC Berkeley is because I have always wanted to pursue my master’s degree abroad after graduation and I felt it would be a perfect opportunity for me to experience different lifestyles and learn with students from all around the world.”

—Penny, Summer Sessions student

“The enthusiastic discussions among classmates and the professors’ ability to explain complex concepts in an approachable manner made it easier to grasp new knowledge. The warmth and friendliness of my fellow students and instructors made every class feel welcoming.”

—Eason, Summer Sessions student

“I am so grateful to have gained a much broader knowledge of the resources offered by UC Berkeley. Initially, I felt like it would be difficult to find a community and to find help when needed, but I now feel more confident in knowing that there is plenty of support out there.”

—Transfer Edge student

At Berkeley Summer Sessions, we’re dedicated to providing high-quality, innovative education to a diverse community of learners. Whether you’re a visiting student or part of the UC Berkeley family, we invite you to explore our hands-on courses and exceptional academic opportunities.
