Why choose UCSF Health for orthopedic care?

At UCSF Health, we provide expert orthopedic care tailored to your needs, including spine care, sports medicine, joint replacement surgery and arthritis therapies.

Whether you have an injury or chronic joint pain, our orthopedic specialists offer nonsurgical and surgical treatments to get you back to your regular activities.

Our orthopedic services

At UCSF Health, you receive personalized care from providers with expertise in the condition or injury you have. Our experienced team includes:

  • Orthopedic surgeons

  • Physiatrists (physical medicine specialists)

  • Sports medicine physicians

  • Women's sports medicine specialists

  • Pediatric orthopedic surgeons

  • Spine specialists

We use the latest diagnostics, including electrodiagnostic testing, to help us create a tailored care plan and track your progress.

Orthopedic care we provide

Your care plan may include:

  • Sports medicine services. Sports medicine providers care for athletes of all ages and skill levels. We treat injuries with a customized care plan that helps you recover quickly and prevent re-injury.

  • Spine care. Our world-renowned spine specialists offer effective treatments with outstanding outcomes. You receive complete spine care, including top-tier diagnostic imaging, pain management, physical therapy and surgery.

  • Orthotics and prosthetics. We offer custom prosthetics (artificial limbs) and orthotics (limb supports) to keep you moving and active.

  • Orthopedic surgery. Our skilled orthopedic surgeons use minimally invasive techniques, which typically lead to less scarring and a quicker recovery. We routinely perform complex bone, joint and ligament surgeries, helping you regain mobility.

  • Orthopedic oncology care. Our surgical oncologists diagnose and treat benign (noncancerous) and malignant (cancerous) bone and soft tissue tumors.

  • Foot and ankle services. Our experts diagnose and treat foot and ankle problems with nonsurgical and surgical treatments, including custom orthotics.

  • Upper extremity care. You receive targeted care from providers specializing in conditions affecting the hands, wrists, elbows, arms and shoulders.
